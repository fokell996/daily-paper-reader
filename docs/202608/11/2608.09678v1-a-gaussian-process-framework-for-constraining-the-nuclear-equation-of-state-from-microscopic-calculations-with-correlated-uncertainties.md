## 摘要
我们基于手征核子-核子和三核子相互作用，在零温下从微观非对称物质计算中给出了核态方程（EOS）的约束。这些约束包括饱和点、不可压缩性的同位旋依赖性、对称能，以及中子星物质的壳-芯过渡密度。为了量化和传播从含噪声的多体计算到导出可观测量之间的相关不确定性，我们引入了GPDiff——一个基于JAX的高效Python包，用于具有自动微分的多元高斯过程（GP）回归。训练后，GPDiff能够对EOS及关于输入变量的任意阶导数（包括混合偏导数）进行联合预测。在此初步应用中，我们分析了近期关于非对称物质的高阶多体微扰理论计算，最高约两倍饱和密度，并探索了用于建模EOS的非平稳变面核（change-surface kernels）——一类依赖于输入的核函数。GPDiff广泛适用于零温和有限温度下的微观核EOS计算，为基于GP的核EOS不确定性量化与推断提供了一个通用工具包。

## Abstract
We present constraints on the nuclear equation of state (EOS) from microscopic asymmetric matter calculations at zero temperature based on chiral nucleon-nucleon and three-nucleon interactions. The constraints include the saturation point, the isospin dependence of the incompressibility, and the symmetry energy, as well as the crust-core transition density of neutron-star matter. To quantify and propagate correlated uncertainties from noisy many-body calculations to derived observables, we introduce GPDiff, an efficient JAX-based Python package for multivariate Gaussian process (GP) regression with automatic differentiation. After training, GPDiff enables joint predictions of the EOS and derivatives of arbitrary order with respect to the input variables, including mixed partial derivatives. In this initial application, we analyze recent high-order many-body perturbation theory calculations of asymmetric matter up to about twice saturation density and explore nonstationary change-surface kernels, a class of input-dependent kernels, for modeling the EOS. GPDiff is broadly applicable to microscopic nuclear EOS calculations at zero and finite temperature and provides a versatile package for GP-based uncertainty quantification and inference of the nuclear EOS.
