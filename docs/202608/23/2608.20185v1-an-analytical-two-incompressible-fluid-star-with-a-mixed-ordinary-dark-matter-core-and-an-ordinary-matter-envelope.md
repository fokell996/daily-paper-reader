## 摘要
我们构建了一个解析的相对论双流体恒星，其特征是一个混合核心，其中普通物质和暗物质作为两种独立守恒的不可压缩完美流体共存，以及一个完全由普通物质组成的外壳。这两种流体既不交换物质也不交换能量，仅通过共同的时空几何相互作用，其中普通组分跨越核心-外壳界面延伸，而暗组分则被限制在核心内。尽管存在混合核心-单流体外壳结构和内部界面，该系统仍然保持解析可处理性，使我们能够获得压力和度规函数的显式表达式，并直接追踪暗物质比例和相对核心大小的影响。我们确定了物理上允许的参数空间，并推导出一个类似布赫达尔的临界致密度，它与中心压力的发散相关，其值取决于相对暗物质密度和混合核心的大小。施瓦西常数密度恒星及其标准临界值$2M/R=8/9$在相应的单流体极限中得以恢复。质量-半径分析进一步表明，具有相同全局致密度的构型可能对应不同的内部物质分布。除了提供对核心受限第二组分的解析控制描述外，该构建还为识别定性趋势提供了一个有用的基准，这些趋势随后可以在更现实的暗物质混合中子星模型中进行检验，其详细处理超出了当前工作的范围。

## Abstract
We construct an analytical relativistic two fluid star characterized by a mixed core, where ordinary matter and dark matter coexist as two independently conserved incompressible perfect fluids, and an envelope composed exclusively of ordinary matter. The fluids exchange neither matter nor energy and interact only through the common spacetime geometry, with the ordinary component extending across the core envelope interface while the dark component is confined to the core. Despite the mixed core--single-fluid envelope structure and the internal interface, the system remains analytically tractable, allowing us to obtain explicit expressions for the pressures and metric functions and to follow directly the effects of the dark-matter fraction and relative core size. We determine the physically admissible parameter space and derive a Buchdahl like critical compactness associated with the divergence of the central pressure, whose value depends on the relative dark matter density and the size of the mixed core. The Schwarzschild constant density star and its standard critical value, $2M/R=8/9$, are recovered in the corresponding one fluid limit. The mass--radius analysis further shows that configurations with the same global compactness can correspond to distinct internal matter distributions. Beyond providing an analytically controlled description of a core-confined second component, the construction offers a useful benchmark for identifying qualitative trends that may subsequently be examined in more realistic dark matter admixed neutron star models, whose detailed treatment lies beyond the scope of the present work.
