## 摘要
随机引力波背景在宇宙流体流动中产生剪切，通过非线性模式耦合，在曲率密度场中产生大尺度白噪声（LSWN）。基于我们先前工作的LSWN框架，我们推导了这种引力波诱导的LSWN的振幅，并将LSWN的观测未探测转化为对引力波产生红移和密度的约束。特别地，对在红移z_*处产生、频率带f_*内z=0密度参数Ω_GW0^*的引力波，最小约束必须满足{z_*}^2 Ω_GW0^* < 5×10^7 (f_*/nHz)^{3/2}。例如，这排除了最近由脉冲星计时阵列探测到的引力波（NANOGrav:2023hvm）在z_*~10^8之前存在，远在夸克-强子相变之后。虽然这比其他引力波约束强几个数量级，但这是最小的LSWN约束，因为对早期宇宙引力波产生的现实建模，包括引力波源的粒状性和相关声波产生的LSWN，可能会将这个约束收紧几个数量级。

## Abstract
A stochastic gravitational wave (GW) background sources a shear in the flow of cosmic fluid which, through non-linear mode coupling, generates large-scale white noise (LSWN) in the kurvature density field. Building on the LSWN framework of our previous work, we derive the amplitude of this GW-induced LSWN and translate the observational non-detection of LSWN into bounds on the production redshift and density of gravity waves. In particular, a minimal constraint on gravity waves with $z=0$ density parameter $Ω_\mathrm{GW0}^*$ in frequency band $f_*$ generated at redshift $z_*$ must satisfy ${z_*}^2\,Ω_\mathrm{GW0}^*<5\times10^7\,(f_*/\mathrm{nHz})^{3/2}$. This, for example, precludes the gravity waves recently detected by pulsar timing arrays \cite{NANOGrav:2023hvm} from being present before $z_*\sim10^8$, long after the quark hadron phase transition. While orders of magnitude stronger than other constraints on gravity waves, this is a minimal LSWN constraint as realistic modeling of early universe gravity wave production, including the granularity of the gravity-wave sources and the LSWN produced by the associated acoustic waves would probably tighten this constraint by orders of magnitude.
