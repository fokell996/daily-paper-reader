## 摘要
在一阶近似下，双中子星引力波（GW）信号对个体自旋 $\chi_1$ 和 $\chi_2$ 的依赖仅通过有效自旋参数 $\chi_{\rm eff}$ 体现。我们首次系统研究了固定总质量、质量比和 $\chi_{\rm eff}$ 条件下个体自旋效应对抛射物、r-过程核合成及千新星辐射的影响。我们使用来自三个总质量区间的数值相对论抛射物，采用有限温度、成分相关的SFHo状态方程，并考虑中微子发射与吸收。对于固定 $\chi_{\rm eff}=0$ 的 $M_{\rm tot}=2.55\,M_\odot$ 模型，个体自旋使动力学抛射物质量变化约45倍，而绝对 $A\geq140$ 产额跨越两个多数量级，镧系元素与轻r-过程物质的质量比从约2增加到约70。快速坍缩的 $4.10\,M_\odot$ 模型在 $\chi_{\rm eff}=0$ 时重元素产额差异超过四个数量级。千新星保留了个体自旋的印记，峰值亮度差异可达约0.9星等。在 $40\,{\rm Mpc}$ 距离处，所有三对固定 $\chi_{\rm eff}=0$ 模型在常见历元对所有观测角度均保持在所采用的探测深度之上，同时同历元颜色差异可达约1.5星等。当模拟导出的长期抛射物被相同的参数化盘外流取代时，颜色印记仍然存在，这表明盘质量差异并非主要驱动因素，动力学抛射物做出了重要贡献。中微子吸收系统地使千新星变亮，并使峰值相关颜色蓝移。这些电磁特征可以打破GW信号中个体自旋之间的简并。

## Abstract
To leading order, the gravitational-wave (GW) signal from binary neutron stars depends on the individual spins, $χ_1$ and $χ_2$, only through the effective spin parameter $χ_{\rm eff}$. We present the first systematic investigation of individual-spin effects on ejecta, $r$-process nucleosynthesis, and kilonova emission, including comparisons at fixed total mass, mass ratio and $χ_{\rm eff}$. We use numerical relativity ejecta from three total mass regimes with the finite-temperature, composition-dependent SFHo equation of state and neutrino emission and absorption. For $M_{\rm tot}=2.55\,M_\odot$ at fixed $χ_{\rm eff}=0$, individual spins change the dynamical ejecta mass by a factor of ${\sim}45$, while the absolute $A\geq140$ yield spans more than two orders of magnitude and the lanthanide to light $r$-process mass ratio increases from ${\sim}2$ to ${\sim}70$. Prompt-collapse $4.10\,M_\odot$ models show heavy-element yield differences exceeding four orders of magnitude at $χ_{\rm eff}=0$. The kilonova retains the individual-spin imprint, with peak brightness differences reaching ${\sim}0.9$ mag. At $40\,{\rm Mpc}$, all three fixed $χ_{\rm eff}=0$ pairs remain above adopted depths at common epochs for all viewing angles, with same-epoch colour differences reaching ${\sim}1.5$ mag. The colour imprint persists when the simulation-derived secular ejecta are replaced by the same parametric disc outflow, indicating that disc mass differences are not the primary driver and that dynamical ejecta make an important contribution. Neutrino absorption systematically brightens the kilonova and shifts peak-associated colours blueward. These EM signatures can break the degeneracy between individual spins in the GW signal.
