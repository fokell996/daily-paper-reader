## 摘要
我们扩展了在研讨会“从夸克到中子星：千赫兹引力波的洞见”中提出的工作，讨论了相对论恒星转动惯量、潮汐形变度和自旋诱导四极矩的一些极限情形。首先，我们推测长度尺度的层级结构是这些二阶矩之间拟议普适性的关键，因此重新审视了不可压缩相对论恒星（即史瓦西内部解）的关联，将其作为可能的最硬极限候选。其次，我们给出了弱场极限下的极限形式。特别地，我们展示了潮汐形变的相对论计算如何与传统的牛顿对应相联系，这一点可能在文献中未被明确呈现过。

## Abstract
Extending the work presented in a workshop ``From Quarks to Neutron Stars: Insights from kHz gravitational waves'', we discuss some limiting cases of the moment of inertia, tidal deformability, and spin-induced quadrupole moment for relativistic stars. First, conjecturing that a hierarchy of the length scale is the key to proposed universality among these second-order moments, we revisit the relation for incompressible relativistic stars (known as Schwarzschild's interior solution) as a candidate of the possible stiff limit. Second, we present the limiting form for the weak-field limit. In particular, we demonstrate how relativistic computations of tidal deformation are related to the traditional Newtonian counterpart, which might not have been presented explicitly in the literature.
