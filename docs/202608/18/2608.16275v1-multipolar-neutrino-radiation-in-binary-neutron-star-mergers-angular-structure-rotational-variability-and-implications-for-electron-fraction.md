## 摘要
使用完全广义相对论模拟，结合能量积分M1中微子输运，对双中子星合并中微子发射的角结构和时间变率进行了表征，这些模拟涵盖了具有代表性的状态方程、总质量和质量比集合。在残余外部的球面上提取角度相关的中微子能量通量，并将其分解为球谐函数以量化其多极含量和演化。在初始合并后瞬变之后，中微子辐射通量趋于轴对称构型，由强四极分量主导，产生持续的极区通量增强和赤道抑制，这是由环影造成的。偶极贡献保持次要地位，表明不存在持续的单侧发射。各向异性程度随着质量不对称性和较软的状态方程而增加，反映了残余-盘系统的致密性和形态。在这种时间平均几何之上，叠加了与$m=1$模式相关的相干方位角调制。傅里叶分析揭示了约0.6–0.7 kHz的特征频率，与残余和内盘层的差分旋转一致，表明旋转结构与中微子发射变率之间存在动力学耦合。最后，我们量化了相同的四极主导辐射几何如何导致纬度依赖的平衡电子丰度。极区物质被驱动接近中微子平衡目标，而赤道物质则系统地保持更富含中子并低于平衡。

## Abstract
The angular structure and temporal variability of neutrino emission from binary neutron star mergers are characterized using fully general-relativistic simulations with energy-integrated M1 neutrino transport across a representative set of equations of state, total masses, and mass ratios. The angle-dependent neutrino energy flux is extracted on a spherical surface outside the remnant and decomposed into spherical harmonics to quantify its multipolar content and evolution. Following the initial post-merger transient, the neutrino radiation flux approaches an axisymmetric configuration dominated by a strong quadrupolar component, producing persistent polar flux enhancement and equatorial suppression due to torus shadowing. The dipolar contribution remains subdominant, indicating the absence of sustained one-sided emission. The degree of anisotropy increases with mass asymmetry and for softer equations of state, reflecting the compactness and morphology of the remnant--disk system. Superimposed on this time-averaged geometry, coherent azimuthal modulations associated with the $m=1$ mode are identified. Fourier analysis reveals a characteristic frequency of $\sim 0.6$--$0.7$ kHz, consistent with differential rotation in the remnant and inner disk layers, indicating a dynamical coupling between rotational structure and neutrino emission variability. Finally, we quantify how the same quadrupole-dominated radiation geometry induces a latitude-dependent equilibrium electron fraction. The polar material is driven close to the neutrino-equilibrium target, whereas equatorial material remains systematically more neutron-rich and below equilibrium.
