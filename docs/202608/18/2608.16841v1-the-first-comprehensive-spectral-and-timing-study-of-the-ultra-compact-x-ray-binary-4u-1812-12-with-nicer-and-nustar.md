## 摘要
源4U 1812-12是一个包含中子星的持续、弱变低质量X射线双星。该源在2019年至2021年间被NICER观测，最近又于2025年被NuSTAR观测。在NICER和NuSTAR观测期间，该源被探测到处于硬谱态，其热光度约为1.90×10^36 erg s^-1。其3-70 keV的NuSTAR光谱特征包括来自吸积盘的软热辐射、来自冕的硬康普顿化辐射及其在吸积盘上的反射。NuSTAR能谱清晰显示出盘反射特征，使用自洽的相对论反射模型relxill进行拟合。我们的反射模拟表明，吸积盘具有中等电离度（log ξ~2.72），延伸至接近中子星表面（R_in ≲ 1.72 R_ISCO），且观测倾角较小（i~25度）。假设磁场（B）截断了吸积盘，我们发现B ≲ 2.54×10^8 G，与中子星低质量X射线双星（NS LMXBs）中观测到的典型值相当。1.0-9.5 keV的NICER光谱也表现为软热成分和占主导的硬康普顿化成分。在NICER观测期间，盘温度在小范围内变化，约为0.69-0.84 keV。相比之下，幂律光子指数Γ变化较大，约为0.8-1.5，这意味着康普顿化发射发生了显著变化。此外，NICER时变分析揭示了宽带无周期变异性，在0.379±0.008 Hz和0.724±0.025 Hz处存在显著的类似QPO的特征，其分数均方根振幅分别为2.9±0.6%和4.1±0.5%。

## Abstract
The source 4U 1812-12 is a persistent, weakly variable low-mass X-ray binary containing a neutron star. The source was observed by NICER between 2019 and 2021 and, more recently, by NuSTAR in 2025. During the NICER and NuSTAR observations, the source was detected in a hard spectral state with a bolometric luminosity of $\sim 1.90\times 10^{36}$ ergs s$^{-1}$. Its $3-70$ keV NuSTAR spectrum is characterized by a soft thermal emission from the disc, a hard Comptonized emission from the corona, and its reflection from the accretion disc. The NuSTAR energy spectrum exhibits the clear presence of disc reflection features, fitted using a self-consistent relativistic reflection model {\tt relxill}. Our reflection modeling indicates a moderately ionized accretion disc (log\:$ξ\sim2.72$) extending close to the neutron star surface ($R_{in}\lesssim 1.72\:R_{ISCO}$), and viewed through a small inclination angle ($i\sim 25$ degrees). Assuming that the magnetic field ($B$) truncates the disc, we found $B\lesssim 2.54\times 10^{8}$ G, comparable to the typical values observed for NS LMXBs. The $1.0-9.5$ keV NICER spectra are also characterized by a soft thermal component and a dominant hard Comptonized component. During NICER observations, the disc temperature exhibits a small variation within $\sim 0.69-0.84$ keV. In contrast, the power law photon index, $Γ$, exhibits a large variation of $\sim 0.8-1.5$, implying a substantial change in the Comptonized emission. Moreover, NICER timing analysis reveals broadband aperiodic variability with significant QPO-like features at $0.379\pm 0.008$ Hz and $0.724\pm 0.025$ Hz, having fractional rms amplitudes of $2.9\pm 0.6\%$ and $4.1\pm 0.5\%$, respectively.
