## 摘要
与中子星质量-半径关系不同（后者通过稠密核物质的状态方程敏感地依赖于内部恒星结构），某些中子星性质近似服从普适关系，这些关系对状态方程的依赖性较弱。在本文中，我们探索了磁化中子星的新的普适关系。我们专注于纯极向偶极磁场位形，并将磁场效应微扰处理至场强的二阶。在适当通过恒星质量和极点处磁场强度归一化相关量后，我们首先确定了磁偶极矩与恒星致密度之间的精确普适关系。然后，我们发现了磁偶极矩、磁致恒星四极矩和椭率之间的近似普适关系，其由状态方程引起的分数变化处于O(10%)水平。我们通过牛顿多方球的解析估计支持这些数值发现，这些估计再现了观察到的行为。在新发现的关系中，连接磁偶极矩和恒星四极矩的关系可能对分析包含磁化中子星的双星引力波信号特别有用。

## Abstract
Unlike the neutron-star mass--radius relation, which depends sensitively on the internal stellar structure through the equation of state of dense nuclear matter, certain neutron-star properties obey approximately universal relations that exhibit only weak dependence on the equation of state. In this paper, we explore new universal relations for magnetized neutron stars. We focus on a purely poloidal dipolar magnetic-field configuration and treat the effects of the magnetic field perturbatively to second order in the field strength. After appropriately normalizing the relevant quantities by the stellar mass and the magnetic-field strength at the pole, we first identify an exact universal relation between the magnetic dipole moment and stellar compactness. We then find approximate universal relations among the magnetic dipole moment, magnetically-induced stellar quadrupole moment, and ellipticity, with fractional variations due to the equation of state at the level of O(10%). We support these numerical findings with analytic estimates for Newtonian polytropes, which reproduce the observed behavior. Among the newly discovered relations, the one connecting the magnetic dipole moment and stellar quadrupole moment may be particularly useful for the analysis of gravitational-wave signals from binaries containing magnetized neutron stars.
