## 摘要
暂现X射线脉冲星（XRPs）爆发末期以质量吸积率显著下降为特征，为理解吸积盘物理及其与中子星（NS）强磁场的相互作用提供了宝贵见解。特别是，“螺旋桨效应”或离心抑制吸积，被认为是控制爆发光度和快速过渡到静止状态时标的关键机制。此外，它还提供了一种估算中子星磁场强度的独立方法。另一方面，质量吸积率的下降本身是由距中子星更远的吸积流中发生的过程所驱动的。因此，恢复光变曲线中编码的信息需要灵敏的高频X射线监测，以捕捉从吸积状态到静止状态的快速且通常不可预测的过渡。在本研究中，我们展示了首个综合监测活动的结果，该活动利用NICER X射线望远镜对暂现XRP 4U 0115+63向静止状态的整个过渡进行了追踪。我们表明，观测到的行为可以用热-粘性盘不稳定性模型（DIM）解释，爆发后立即观测到的发射可能源于重组（“冷”）盘的持续吸积，而随后的静止发射则由冷却中的中子星产生。我们进一步将该模型应用于涵盖广泛物理参数的更大样本。最终，我们的发现表明，XRPs的时间行为，包括静止状态，可以在DIM框架内得到一致解释，而无需将螺旋桨效应作为控制观测过渡的主要机制。

## Abstract
The final stages of outbursts in transient X-ray pulsars (XRPs), which are characterised by a significant decline in the mass accretion rate, provide valuable insight into the physics of the accretion disc and its interaction with the strong magnetic field of the neutron star (NS). In particular, the `propeller effect', or centrifugal inhibition of accretion, has been proposed as a key mechanism governing both the onset luminosity and the timescale of the rapid transition to the quiescent state. In addition, it offers an independent method for estimating the magnetic field strength of the NS. On the other hand, the decrease in the mass accretion rate itself is driven by processes occurring in the accretion flow at larger distances from the NS. Recovering the information encoded in the light curve therefore requires sensitive high-cadence X-ray monitoring capable of capturing the rapid and often unpredictable transition from the accreting regime to the quiescent regime. In this study, we present the results of the first comprehensive monitoring campaign that tracks the entire transition to quiescence in the transient XRP 4U 0115+63 utilising observations by the NICER X-ray telescope. We show that the observed behaviour can be explained by the thermal-viscous disc instability model (DIM), with the emission observed immediately after an outburst possibly arising from the ongoing accretion from the recombined (`cold') disc and the subsequent quiescent emission being produced by the cooling NS. We further applied this model to a larger sample of XRPs encompassing a broad range of physical parameters. Ultimately, our findings indicate that the temporal behaviour of XRPs, including the quiescent state, can be consistently explained within the DIM framework without requiring the propeller effect as the primary mechanism governing the observed transition.
