## 摘要
奇异夸克物质（SQM）假说认为，由SQM构成的天体可能存在于从奇异行星（SPs）到奇异星（SSs）的广泛质量范围内。已有研究提出，螺旋靠近的SS-SP系统发出的引力波（GWs）可能被地面引力波天文台如先进LIGO和爱因斯坦望远镜探测到。然而，此类系统在进入螺旋阶段之前，可能在紧密构型中经历长时间的轨道演化。在此期间，它可以产生频率范围从毫赫兹（mHz）到分赫兹（dHz）的连续引力波信号。这些引力波的详细特征尚未被深入研究。在本研究中，我们探讨了SS-SP系统的连续引力波特征，重点是探索物理上可行的参数空间。我们将这些系统发出的引力波信号与下一代天基引力波探测器如分赫兹干涉仪引力波天文台（DECIGO）和大爆炸观测者（BBO）的灵敏度曲线进行了比较。我们的分析表明，DECIGO和BBO探测器都能够在广泛的参数空间内探测到来自SS-SP系统的连续引力波。这些引力波携带了检验SQM假说的重要信息，也有助于推进我们对超新星和致密星并合过程的理解。

## Abstract
The Strange Quark Matter (SQM) hypothesis posits that objects composed of SQM could exist across a wide mass range, from strange planets (SPs) to strange stars (SSs). It has been proposed that gravitational waves (GWs) emitted by inspiraling SS-SP systems may be detectable by ground-based GW observatories such as advanced LIGO and the Einstein Telescope. Nevertheless, such a system may undergo an extended period of orbital evolution in a close configuration before entering the inspiraling phase. During this time, it can generate continuous GW signals at frequencies ranging from milli-hertz (mHz) to deci-hertz (dHz). The detailed characteristics of these GWs have not yet been thoroughly explored. In this study, we delve into the continuous GW features of SS-SP systems, with a focus on exploring the physically viable parameter space. We compared the GW signals emitted by these systems to the sensitivity curves of next-generation space-based GW detectors like the Deci-hertz Interferometer Gravitational wave Observatory (DECIGO) and the Big Bang Observer (BBO). Our analyses demonstrate that both the DECIGO and BBO detectors are capable of detecting continuous GWs from SS-SP systems across a broad parameter space. These GWs carry important information for testing the SQM hypothesis, as well as for advancing our understanding of supernovae and compact star merger processes.
