## 摘要
双中子星并合事件GW170817及其电磁对应体GRB170817A和AT2017gfo的多信使观测标志着天体物理学的一个里程碑，但其统一物理解释仍难以捉摸。我们基于第一性原理的广义相对论磁流体动力学中微子辐射输运并合模拟，结合核合成计算和光子辐射输运生成千新星光变曲线，进行了端到端模拟。我们表明，大尺度发电机同时产生各向同性能光度约为$\sim 10^{51}~{\rm erg~s^{-1}}$的相对论性喷流和约$0.08M_\odot$的富中子抛射物，重现了GRB170817A余辉和AT2017gfo千新星光变曲线。我们的结果为跨引力波、伽马射线暴和千新星观测解释双中子星并合建立了统一的第一性原理框架。

## Abstract
The multimessenger observation of the binary neutron star merger event GW170817, associated with its electromagnetic counterparts GRB170817A and AT2017gfo, marked a milestone in astrophysics, yet its unified physical explanation remains elusive. We conduct an end-to-end simulation based on a first-principles general-relativistic magnetohydrodynamics neutrino-radiation transfer merger simulation, followed by nucleosynthesis calculations and photon radiative transfer to generate kilonova light curves. We show that the large-scale dynamo simultaneously produces a relativistic jet with an isotropic-equivalent luminosity of $\sim 10^{51}~{\rm erg~s^{-1}}$ and $\approx 0.08M_\odot$ of neutron-rich ejecta, reproducing the GRB170817A afterglow and the AT2017gfo kilonova light curves. Our results establish a unified first-principles framework for interpreting binary neutron star mergers across gravitational wave, gamma-ray burst, and kilonova observations.
