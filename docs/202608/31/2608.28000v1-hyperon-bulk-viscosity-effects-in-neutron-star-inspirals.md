## 摘要
本文重新审视了中子星并合过程中超子体黏滞的作用。我们发现超子体黏滞对引力波相位的影响很小，引起的相移约为$\sim 10^{-3}\,\rm rad$。这个相移太小，现有的引力波天文台无法探测到，而且下一代探测器似乎也不太可能将这一效应与其他因素分离开来。然而，我们的分析表明，超子体黏滞可以显著加热中子星的超子核心，将其温度提高到约$5 \times 10^8\,\rm K$。

## Abstract
The paper revisits the role of hyperon bulk viscosity during neutron-star inspiral. We find that hyperon bulk viscosity exerts only a minor influence on the gravitational-wave phase, causing a phase shift of $\sim 10^{-3}\,\rm rad$. This shift is too small to be detected by existing gravitational-wave observatories, and it seems unlikely that next-generation detectors will be able to isolate this effect from other factors. However, our analysis indicates that hyperon bulk viscosity can significantly heat the hyperon core of a neutron star, raising its temperature to approximately $5 \times 10^8\,\rm K$.
