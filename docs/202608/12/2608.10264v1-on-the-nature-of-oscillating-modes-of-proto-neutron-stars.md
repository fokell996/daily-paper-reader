## 摘要
新生原中子星（PNS）是引力波星震学（通过恒星振荡模式研究其内部结构）的潜在目标。为了为当前和未来引力波观测站最终探测到这些模式做好准备，理论研究已获得了可能的振荡谱。然而，在根据激发这些模式的物理机制来识别和分类具体模式方面，一直存在分歧。在本文中，我们提出了一种新的方案，用于对核心坍缩超新星（CCSNe）中被激波包围的新生PNS的振荡模式进行分类。我们的分类具有物理动机，因为它基于模式恢复力能量。我们通过考虑CCSNe的不同区域来研究这些模式的性质，这些模式源于这些区域。我们将此方案应用于28个非旋转的1D、2D和3D CCSN模拟，这些模拟使用两种不同的数值代码以及不同的前身星和状态方程。通过这种方式，我们发现在系统中存在不同的f、p和g模式族，它们存在于不同区域。具体来说，我们识别出两个分别与PNS和激波相关的f和p模式族，以及两个起源于两个对流稳定区域的g模式族，一个在PNS核心，另一个在其表面附近。我们还发现，主导的高频发射模式是PNS的f模式，与其表面的强密度梯度相关。我们的分类程序是自动的，对所有考虑的模型都表现出一致性，并为系统研究模式频率对PNS性质的依赖铺平了道路，可直接应用于未来引力波信号观测的参数估计。

## Abstract
Newborn proto-neutron stars (PNS) are potential targets of gravitational wave asteroseismology, the study of the (inner) structure of stars via their oscillation modes. To prepare for the eventual detection of such modes by current and future gravitational wave observatories, theoretical studies have obtained the possible spectrum of oscillations. However, there has been disagreement when it comes to identifying and classifying specific modes according to the physical mechanism that excites them. In this paper, we present a novel scheme to classify the oscillation modes of a newly born PNS surrounded by a stalled accretion shock in core-collapse supernovae (CCSNe). Our classification is physically motivated, as it is based on the energy of the restoring forces of the mode. We investigate the nature of the modes by considering the different regions of the CCSN that they stem from. We apply this scheme to a set of 28 non-rotating 1D, 2D and 3D CCSN simulations performed with two different numerical codes and using different progenitors and equations of state. In that way, we find that there are different families of f-, p- and g-modes in the system, living in different areas. More specifically, we identify two families of f- and p-modes associated with the PNS and the shock, respectively, and two families of g-modes originating from the two convectively stable regions, one in the PNS core and one near its surface. We also find that the dominant high-frequency emission mode is the f-mode of the PNS, associated with the strong density gradient at its surface. Our classification procedure is automatic, performs consistently for all the models considered, and paves the way to systematic studies of the dependence of the mode frequencies on the PNS properties, with direct application to parameter estimation from future observations of gravitational wave signals.
