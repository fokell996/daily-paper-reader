## 摘要
大质量星系团可以在视线方向上引入引力透镜效应和恒星形成被抑制的成员星系族群，可能改变朝向它们可观测源相对于平均天空的分布。因此，与星系团对齐的快速射电暴（FRB）可以提供一个独特的窗口，进入其他视线方向无法提供的FRB族群参数空间。在本研究中，我们证明第二个CHIME/FRB基带目录包含从最新DECaLS数据发布中识别出的星系团内部或背后的FRB；我们分离出26个可能存在这种情况的FRB样本，包括一个重复FRB和两个穿过后发座星系团的FRB。将我们的结果与一系列模拟FRB族群进行比较，我们得出结论，这些关联的数量代表朝向星系团的$3σ$速率增强，构成第二个CHIME/FRB基带目录中检测到的FRB的$1.4\pm0.4\%$。我们认为这种增强是由相同比例的成员星系宿主额外FRB以及引力透镜放大背景源造成的。我们证明这种贡献对FRB族群中的替代前身通道和高红移演化敏感，为约束这些特征提供了未来途径。考虑到相关星系团的红移和质量，我们识别出FRB 20211113A，它与强引力透镜Abell 2218对齐，作为潜在透镜候选体供进一步考虑。

## Abstract
Massive galaxy clusters can introduce gravitational lensing and populations of suppressed star formation member galaxies into the line of sight, potentially changing the distribution of observable sources toward them from that of the average sky. As a result, Fast Radio Bursts (FRBs) aligned with galaxy clusters can provide a unique window into parameter spaces of the FRB population that other lines of sight do not afford. In this study, we demonstrate that the second CHIME/FRB baseband catalog contains FRBs emitted from within or behind clusters identified from the latest DECaLS data release; we isolate a sample of 26 FRBs where this is likely, including one repeating FRB and two FRBs that intersect the Coma cluster. Comparing our results against a range of simulated FRB populations, we conclude that the number of these associations represents a $3σ$ rate enhancement toward galaxy clusters, constituting $1.4\pm0.4\%$ of the FRBs detected in the second CHIME/FRB baseband catalog. We suggest that this enhancement is caused by an equal proportion of member galaxies hosting additional FRBs, and gravitational lensing magnifying background sources. We demonstrate that such contributions are sensitive to alternative progenitor channels and high redshift evolution in the FRB population, providing a future avenue for constraining these features. Considering the redshifts and masses of the associated clusters, we identify FRB 20211113A, which is aligned with the strong gravitational lens Abell 2218 as a potential lensed candidate for further consideration.

  - 透镜模型仅基于少量高星系团外推，低质量透镜贡献可能被低估。
