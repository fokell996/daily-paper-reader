## 摘要
双中子星模拟中恒星表面的处理对于数值演化的精度和预测可观测量的物理可靠性至关重要。与恒星表面附近陡峭梯度处理相关的数值伪影可能在旋进期间产生伪加热，导致内部能量的人为增加和中子星的非物理膨胀。在本工作中，我们研究了基于熵的通量限制（EFL）方案在有限差分代码BAM中减轻这些数值效应的有效性。我们采用一组代表性的混合状态方程，对孤立中子星和双中子星旋进进行了模拟。我们表明，EFL方案显著减少了人为表面加热。这种减少在所有考虑的恒星模型和双星构型中一致地观察到，表明数值加热的减少是EFL方法的一个稳健特征。

## Abstract
The treatment of the stellar surface in binary neutron star simulations is crucial for the accuracy of the numerical evolution and the physical reliability of the predicted observables. Numerical artifacts associated with the treatment of steep gradients near the stellar surface can produce spurious heating during the inspiral, leading to an artificial increase of the internal energy and an unphysical expansion of the neutron star. In this work, we investigate the effectiveness of the entropy-based flux-limiting (EFL) scheme in mitigating these numerical effects within the finite-difference code BAM. We perform simulations of both isolated neutron stars and binary neutron star inspirals employing a representative set of hybrid equations of state. We show that the EFL scheme significantly reduces artificial surface heating. This reduction is observed consistently across all stellar models and binary configurations considered, demonstrating that the reduction of numerical heating is a robust feature of the EFL method.
