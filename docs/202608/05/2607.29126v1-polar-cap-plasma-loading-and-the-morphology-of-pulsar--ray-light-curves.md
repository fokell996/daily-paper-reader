## 摘要
费米大面积望远镜（Fermi-LAT）发现了超过300颗γ射线脉冲星，揭示了丰富多样的光变曲线形态，这难以在纯磁层辐射模型中重现，表明粒子加速和辐射可能至少部分发生在条带风电流片中。我们基于分裂单极子电流片几何结构计算了一个全面的脉冲星γ射线光变曲线图谱，旨在量化对等离子体加载在塑造观测脉冲形态中的作用。电流片表面通过解析方式描述，并假设为高能光子发射的主要场所。通过一组最小参数引入空间相关的发射率描述，这些参数明确打破了发射等离子体的南北和方位角对称性。仅凭少数几个物理动机明确的参数，该模型就能重现广泛的观测γ射线光变曲线形态，包括不对称、多峰和高度结构化的轮廓。结果表明，不对称发射率（可能与对等离子体加载相关）提供了一种自然且经济机制，能够重现相当大一部分观测到的γ射线脉冲轮廓多样性。因此，电流片场景似乎为高能脉冲星发射提供了一个有前景且物理动机明确的框架。

## Abstract
The discovery of more than 300 $γ$-ray pulsars by the Fermi Large Area Telescope (Fermi-LAT) has revealed a rich diversity of light-curve morphologies that remains challenging to reproduce within purely magnetospheric emission models, suggesting that particle acceleration and radiation may occur, at least in part, in the striped-wind current sheet. We compute a comprehensive atlas of pulsar $γ$-ray light curves based on the split-monopole current sheet geometry, with the goal of quantifying the role of pair plasma loading in shaping the observed pulse morphology. The current-sheet surface is described analytically and assumed to be the dominant site of high-energy photon emission. Spatially dependent emissivity prescriptions are introduced through a minimal set of parameters that explicitly break the north-south and azimuthal symmetries of the emitting plasma. With only a few physically motivated parameters, the model is able to reproduce a broad range of the observed $γ$-ray light-curve morphologies, including asymmetric, multi-peaked, and highly structured profiles. The results show that asymmetric emissivity, possibly related to pair-plasma loading, provides a natural and economical mechanism capable of reproducing a substantial fraction of the observed diversity of $γ$-ray pulse profiles. The current-sheet scenario therefore appears to provide a promising and physically motivated framework for high-energy pulsar emission.
