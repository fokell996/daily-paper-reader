## 摘要
我们首次展示了使用广义相对论磁流体动力学（GRMHD）Spritz代码，采用有限温度表格化状态方程进行的双中子星并合模拟。两个磁化双星系统，采用LS220状态方程和类似GW170817的啁啾质量，仅质量比不同（q=1和q=0.7），从晚期旋近阶段演化经过并合、超质量中子星（HMNS）形成以及延迟坍缩至黑洞（BH）。我们研究了双星质量比如何影响并合后演化、喷流发射条件和物质抛射。我们发现，等质量双星形成寿命稍长的HMNS，使得并合后演化期间磁场的放大更有效。因此，它积累了更大的磁通量，形成了磁主导的漏斗，并发射出准直的磁驱动极向外流。相反，不等质量双星抛射的物质多约50%，但磁场放大较弱，在我们的整个模拟过程中极区保持湍流且重子负载，表明磁主导漏斗的发展可能被延迟。

## Abstract
We present the first binary neutron star merger simulations performed with the general-relativistic magnetohydrodynamics (GRMHD) Spritz code employing a finite-temperature tabulated equation of state. Two magnetized binaries with the LS220 equation of state and a GW170817-like chirp mass, differing only in their mass ratios ($q=1$ and $q=0.7$), are evolved from the late inspiral through merger, hypermassive neutron-star (HMNS) formation, and delayed collapse to a black hole (BH). We investigate how the binary mass ratio influences the postmerger evolution, jet-launching conditions, and matter ejection. We find that the equal-mass binary forms a slightly longer-lived HMNS, allowing more efficient magnetic-field amplification during the post-merger evolution. As a result, it accumulates larger magnetic fluxes, develops a magnetically dominated funnel, and launches a collimated magnetically driven polar outflow. In contrast, the unequal-mass binary ejects approximately 50% more mass but exhibits weaker magnetic-field amplification, with the polar region remaining turbulent and baryon loaded throughout our simulation, suggesting that the development of a magnetically dominated funnel is likely delayed.
