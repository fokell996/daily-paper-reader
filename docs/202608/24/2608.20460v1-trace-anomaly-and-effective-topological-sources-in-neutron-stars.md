## 摘要
本研究探讨了迹异常能否诊断张量多标量引力中恒星对拓扑标量场的响应。我们检查了十一个冷列表状态方程（EoSs），其中六个在广义相对论（GR）热力学和因果性检查后保留。我们在互补匹配规则下将它们的标准拓扑构型与对应的GR模型进行了比较。在控制恒星质量和EoS依赖性后，GR迹源强度$S_T$仍与拓扑质量响应强相关，偏Spearman系数$ρ=0.975$。在接近$1.4\,M_\odot$时，拓扑构型系统性地更致密，Jordan框架半径在固定重子质量下减少$6.7$--$8.7\%$，在固定引力质量下减少$0.87$--$1.29\,\mathrm{km}$。这些偏移与中子星内部组成探测器（NICER）当前的不确定性相当，并产生源依赖的观测效应。尽管存在整体形变，内部有效源仍以物质为主，中位拓扑贡献约为$0.8\%$。因此，GR物质迹成为标准拓扑恒星响应的有用诊断工具。

## Abstract
This work investigates whether the trace anomaly can diagnose the stellar response to a topological scalar field in tensor multi-scalar gravity. Eleven cold tabulated equations of state (EoSs) were examined, with six retained after general relativistic (GR) thermodynamic and causality checks. Their fiducial topological configurations were compared with the corresponding GR models under complementary matching prescriptions. After controlling for stellar mass and EoS dependence, the GR trace source strength $S_T$ remained strongly correlated with the topological mass response, with a partial Spearman coefficient $ρ=0.975$. Near $1.4\,M_\odot$, the topological configurations were systematically more compact, with Jordan frame radius reductions of $6.7$--$8.7\%$ at fixed baryonic mass and $0.87$--$1.29\,\mathrm{km}$ at fixed gravitational mass. These shifts are comparable to current uncertainties from the Neutron Star Interior Composition Explorer (NICER) and produce source dependent observational effects. Despite the global deformation, the interior effective source remained matter dominated, with a median topological contribution of about $0.8\%$. The GR matter trace therefore emerges as a useful diagnostic of the fiducial topological stellar response.
