## 摘要
提出了一种两阶段方法论概念验证，用于在真实LIGO Livingston噪声中筛查简化的合并后引力波阻尼异常。注入信号是一个受控的阻尼正弦代理，而非物理上完整的双中子星合并后波形；它用于测试在真实噪声校准下的统计可检测性和阻尼时间评分。第一阶段从白化候选窗口的恒Q表示构建局部时频图，并使用前导特征值作为可检测性统计量，其零分布在真实探测器噪声上经验校准。第二阶段使用非线性最小二乘和可解释的岭/能量衰减估计器，为通过第一阶段的候选估计有效阻尼时间分数，阈值在最终保留评估之前固定。匹配滤波器信噪比约定使用合成高斯噪声进行检查，而图统计量通过功效比较和瓦片洗牌测试进行评估。在保留的真实噪声测试块上，非线性最小二乘第二阶段评分给出AUC = 0.943，在FPR = 0.012时TPR = 0.879，优于校准的仅功率基线。

## Abstract
A two-stage methodological proof of concept for screening simplified post-merger gravitational-wave damping anomalies in real LIGO Livingston noise is presented. The injected signal is a controlled damped-sinusoid proxy rather than a physically complete binary-neutron-star post-merger waveform; it is used to test statistical detectability and damping-time scoring under real-noise calibration. Stage 1 constructs a local time-frequency graph from Constant-Q representations of whitened candidate windows and uses the leading eigenvalue as a detectability statistic, with its null distribution calibrated empirically on real detector noise. Stage 2 estimates an effective damping-time score for Stage-1-passing candidates using nonlinear least squares and an interpretable ridge/energy-decay estimator, with thresholds fixed before final held-out evaluation. The matched-filter SNR convention is checked with synthetic Gaussian noise, while the graph statistic is assessed through power-comparison and tile-shuffling tests. On a held-out real-noise test block, the nonlinear least-squares Stage-2 score gives AUC = 0.943 and TPR = 0.879 at FPR = 0.012, outperforming a calibrated power-only baseline.
