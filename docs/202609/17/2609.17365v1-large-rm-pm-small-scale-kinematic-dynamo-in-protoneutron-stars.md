## 摘要
磁星是年轻、孤立的中子星，拥有异常强的磁场，其表面偶极磁场强度约为 $10^{15}$ G。产生如此强磁场的一种可能情形是，在原中子星阶段通过湍流对流发电机实现指数放大。然而，对流预期持续时间很短（$\sim 10$ s），这对发电机增长率施加了严格限制。我们在无弹性近似下进行了大量共 82 组三维对流发电机模拟，并研究其运动学阶段，以量化发电机增长率 $\gamma$。我们发现，$\gamma$ 随磁普朗特数 ${\rm Pm}$ 和瑞利数 ${\rm Ra}$ 的增大而增大，且最不稳定模式变得高度非轴对称且多极。我们进一步观察到，随着磁雷诺数 ${\rm Rm}$ 增大，发电机从大尺度逐渐过渡到小尺度，导致磁场主要集中在小尺度上。当改变外部磁场边界条件时，这一趋势保持不变。由于数值上分辨越来越小的尺度变得不切实际，我们采用理论小尺度 Kazantsev 发电机模型来探索原中子星特有的大 ${\rm Pm}$ 区域。该模型定性地再现了模拟中观察到的增长率行为，并且在外推到 大 ${\rm Pm}$ 极限时表明，在原中子星（PNS）中 $\gamma$ 仅弱依赖于电阻率。在与 PNS 相关的条件下，该模型预测磁能增长率约为 $\sim 1$ ms$^{-1}$。

## Abstract
Magnetars are young, isolated neutron stars that possess an exceptionally strong magnetic field, with surface dipolar strengths on the order of $10^{15}$ G. One of the plausible scenarios for generating such a strong field is an exponential amplification by a turbulent convective dynamo during the protoneutron star phase. However, the short expected duration of the convection ($\sim 10$ s) imposes a stringent constraint on the dynamo growth rate. We perform an extensive set of 82 three-dimensional convective dynamo simulations in the anelastic approximation and investigate the kinematic phase to quantify the dynamo growth rate $γ$. We find that $γ$ increases with both the magnetic Prandtl number ${\rm Pm}$ and the Rayleigh number ${\rm Ra}$, with the most unstable mode becoming highly non-axisymmetric and multipolar. We further observe a gradual transition from large-scale to small-scale dynamo as the magnetic Reynolds number ${\rm Rm}$ increases, resulting in a magnetic field that is predominantly concentrated at small scales. The trend remains unchanged when the outer magnetic boundary condition is varied. Since resolving the increasingly small scales becomes numerically impractical, we employ the theoretical small-scale Kazantsev dynamo model to explore the large ${\rm Pm}$ regime characteristic of protoneutron stars. The model qualitatively captures the growth rate behaviour observed in simulations and, upon extrapolation to the large ${\rm Pm}$ limit, indicates that $γ$ is only weakly dependent on the resistivity in a PNS. Under conditions relevant to the PNS, this model predicts a magnetic energy growth rate of the order of $\sim 1$ ms$^{-1}$.
