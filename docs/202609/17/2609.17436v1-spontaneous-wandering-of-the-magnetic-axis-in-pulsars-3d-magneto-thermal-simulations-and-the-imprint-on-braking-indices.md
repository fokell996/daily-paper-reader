## 摘要
对数百颗脉冲星的制动指数测量，尽管存在观测上的注意事项，仍显示出清晰的趋势：在早期年龄时 n<3，在中年期 n>3，而在更大的特征年龄时，数值越来越大且波动。孤立中子星中的壳层磁场通过欧姆耗散和跨尺度的非线性霍尔重分布演化，此外还有手性磁效应和 post-burial re-emergence（埋藏后重现）在早期阶段的额外贡献。初始条件通常被假定为偶极主导，而更复杂且更真实的场——能量在广泛尺度范围内更均匀地分布——尚未在此背景下得到探索。我们评估初始非平庸场的内部动力学如何改变偶极场强度和倾角，以及它们对制动指数 n 的联合贡献（连同轴对齐的贡献）与观测值相比如何。为此，我们进行长期三维磁热模拟，涵盖从偶极主导情形到缠结的、小尺度主导的场的各种构型。具有弱偶极分量的小尺度主导构型表现出丰富的动力学：偶极模不断由小尺度供给，并可能根据初始谱增长或衰减，从而驱动 n<3 或 n>3，而倾角则在短至 ${\cal O}$(kyr) 的时间尺度上变化。这些自发变化常常主导轴对齐力矩，并且能够解释在特征年龄 τ_c\gtrsim10^5 yr 处观测到的符号可正可负的较大 n-3 值，这与偶极主导模型不同。然而，为了定量解释这些测量结果以及结构化的计时残差，可能仍然需要短期磁层波动或超流体驱动的力矩变化。未来将这些模型与观测到的计时特性进行比较的工作，可以微调最有前景的初始构型。

## Abstract
Braking index measurements of hundreds of pulsars, despite observational caveats, show a clear trend: $n<3$ at early ages, $n>3$ at middle ages, and increasingly large, fluctuating values at larger characteristic ages. Crustal magnetic fields in isolated neutron stars evolve through Ohmic dissipation and non-linear Hall redistribution across scales, with additional early-time contributions from the chiral magnetic effect and post-burial re-emergence. Initial conditions are usually assumed dipole-dominated, whereas more complex and realistic fields, with energy spread more evenly over a broad range of scales, have not been explored in this context. We assess how the internal dynamics of initially non-trivial fields change the dipolar field strength and obliquity, and how their combined contribution to the braking index $n$, together with that of the alignment, compares with the observed values. To this end we perform long-term 3D magneto-thermal simulations, for configurations ranging from the dipole-dominated case to tangled, small-scale-dominated fields. Small-scale dominated configurations with weak dipolar components show rich dynamics: the dipolar mode is continuously fed by the small scales and may grow or decay depending on the initial spectrum, driving $n<3$ or $n>3$, while the obliquity varies on timescales as short as ${\cal O}$(kyr). These spontaneous changes often dominate the alignment torque and can account for the large values of $n-3$ of either sign observed at characteristic ages $τ_c\gtrsim10^5$ yr, unlike the dipole-dominated models. However, short-term magnetospheric fluctuations or superfluid-driven variations of the torque may still be needed to explain the measurements and the structured timing residuals quantitatively. Future work comparing these models with the observed timing properties could fine-tune the most promising initial configurations.
