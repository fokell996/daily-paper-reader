## 摘要
我们详细分析了 FRB 20250607A，这是一个由 CRAFT 巡天探测到的双成分快速射电暴，其表现出若干不寻常的成分依赖性质，包括表观差分 DM、圆偏振和随时间分辨的 RM，以及贯穿散射尾部的偏振角（PA）摆动。其散射时标与闪烁带宽与单一薄屏起源不一致，将主导散射屏定位于距 FRB 源 $\lesssim61$ kpc 以内。散射尾部中缺乏 PA 平坦化，这是一种此前未在 FRB 或脉冲星中报告过的行为，且难以用简单的传播起源来解释。尽管两个成分在其色散上存在表观且视觉上明显的差异，我们并未发现两者之间存在统计上显著的差分 DM，这表明当前的不确定度可能过于保守。如果这种表观差分 DM 为真，则意味着两个成分视线之间的电子柱密度存在显著差异。另外两个 CRAFT FRB，FRB 20190611B 和 FRB 20210407E，显示出类似大的成分依赖电子柱差异，对应于与中子星磁层局域等离子体一致的电子密度反差。我们未发现广义法拉第旋转的证据，且 FRB 20210407E 显示出成分依赖的 PA 结构。综合来看，这些结果表明，观测到的 DM、圆偏振和 PA 行为可能源于穿过非均匀或演化磁层的不同视线，而非来自彼此独立的现象。因此，多成分 FRB 为探测其前身星局域等离子体结构提供了灵敏探针。

## Abstract
We present a detailed analysis of FRB 20250607A, a two-component FRB detected by the CRAFT survey which exhibits several unusual component-dependent properties, including apparent differential DM, circular polarisation, and time-resolved RM, together with a polarisation-angle (PA) swing that persists through the scattering tail. The scattering timescale and scintillation bandwidth are inconsistent with a single thin-screen origin, placing the dominant scattering screen within $\lesssim61$ kpc of the FRB source. The lack of PA flattening in the scattering tail is a behaviour not previously reported in an FRB or pulsar and difficult to reconcile with a simple propagation origin. We find no statistically significant differential DM between the two components despite an apparent, and visually obvious, difference in their dispersion, indicating that current uncertainties may be overly conservative. If this apparent differential DM is true, it implies a substantial difference in electron column density between the component sightlines. Two further CRAFT FRBs, FRB 20190611B and FRB 20210407E, show similarly large component-dependent electron-column differences, corresponding to electron-density contrasts consistent with plasma local to a neutron-star magnetosphere. We find no evidence for generalised Faraday rotation, and FRB 20210407E shows component-dependent PA structure. Taken together, these results suggest that the observed DM, circular polarisation, and PA behaviour may arise from differing sightlines through an inhomogeneous or evolving magnetosphere rather than from independent phenomena. Multi-component FRBs therefore provide a sensitive probe of plasma structure local to their progenitors.
