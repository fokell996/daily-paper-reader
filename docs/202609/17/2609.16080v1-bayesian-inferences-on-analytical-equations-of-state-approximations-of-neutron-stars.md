## 摘要
稠密物质的状态方程（EoS）通常以列表形式给出的压强-能量密度关系提供，这使数值实现复杂化，并可能损害热力学一致性。在这项工作中，我们提出一种通用的、分段连续的函数形式，其具有统一参数化，能够表示一大类稠密物质状态方程。我们利用来自 CompOSE 和 LALSimulation 数据库的列表状态方程验证该参数化。在初始确定性拟合之后，我们采用了两种不同的贝叶斯方法：一种基于通过向拟合的状态方程添加噪声而生成的合成状态方程，另一种基于对中子星质量和潮汐形变的多信使测量。在两种方法中，初始最佳拟合参数均被用作参考值。对于所考虑的状态方程，从非常软到非常硬，所得拟合以足够高的精度再现了数据库中定义的列表状态方程及相关的宏观中子星可观测量。推断出的参数表现出强相关性，这些相关性源于在分段边界处施加的连续性条件。在多信使推断下，这些相关性在低密度和中密度分段中持续存在，但在核心区域变弱，反映出当前观测在高密度处有限的约束能力。单一函数形式能够描述源自不同微观框架的状态方程，加之相似参数相关性的反复出现，表明不同的稠密物质状态方程可能共享一种共同的底层结构。

## Abstract
Equations of state (EoS) for dense matter are commonly provided in tabulated pressure-energy density relations, complicating numerical implementation and potentially compromising thermodynamic consistency. In this work, we propose a universal, piecewise-continuous functional form with a common parametrization capable of representing a broad class of dense matter EoSs. We validate this parametrization against tabulated EoSs from the CompOSE and LALSimulation repositories. Following the initial deterministic fitting, we employed two distinct Bayesian approaches: one based on synthetic EoSs generated by adding noise to the fitted EoS and another based on multimessenger measurements of neutron-star masses and tidal deformabilities. In both approaches, the initial best-fit parameters are used as reference values. For the considered EoSs, spanning from very soft to very stiff, the resulting fits reproduce the defined tabulated EoSs and the associated macroscopic neutron-star observables in the repositories with sufficient degree of accuracy. The inferred parameters exhibit strong correlations arising from the continuity conditions imposed at the segment boundaries. These correlations persist in the low- and intermediate-density segments under multimessenger inference but become weak in the core, reflecting the limited constraining power of current observations at high densities. The ability of a single functional form to describe EoSs derived from different microscopic frameworks, together with the recurrence of similar parameter correlations, suggests that diverse dense matter EoSs may share a common underlying structure.
