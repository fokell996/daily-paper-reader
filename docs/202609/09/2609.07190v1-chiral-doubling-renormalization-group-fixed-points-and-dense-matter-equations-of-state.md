## 摘要
我们回顾了一种自上而下的不动点外推范式，作为强子谱、稠密重子物质和致密星物理的统一有效场论框架。通过直接在重正化群（RG）不动点上构建有效拉格朗日量并引入最小对称性破缺，我们将理论外推回物理环境。在真空中，该框架通过轻矢量介子圈自然重现了重-轻介子宇称倍增谱。在致密物质中，手征不变核子质量m0与行走矢量耦合之间的相互作用协调了引力波约束与2倍太阳质量的大质量中子星。推广到夸克-强子混合方法进一步阐明了连续退禁闭、核心稳定性以及重子数涨落，展示了潜在的RG不动点如何主导从真空到中子星内部的强子动力学。

## Abstract
We review a top-down fixed-point-extrapolation paradigm as a unified effective field theory framework for hadronic spectroscopy, dense baryonic matter, and compact star physics. By formulating our effective Lagrangian directly at renormalization group (RG) fixed points and introducing minimal symmetry breaking, we extrapolate the theory back to physical environments. In the vacuum, this framework naturally reproduces the heavy-light meson parity-doubling spectrum via light vector meson loops. In dense matter, the interplay between the chiral-invariant nucleon mass m0 and walking vector couplings reconciles gravitational-wave constraints with $2M_\odot$ massive neutron stars. Generalization to a quark-hadron hybrid approach further illuminates sequential deconfinement, core stability, and baryon number fluctuations, demonstrating how underlying RG fixed points dictate hadronic dynamics from vacuum to neutron star interiors.
