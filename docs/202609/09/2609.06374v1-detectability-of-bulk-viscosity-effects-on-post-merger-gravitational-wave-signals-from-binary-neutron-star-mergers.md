## 摘要
我们研究了体粘滞效应对双中子星并合后引力波信号的影响及其可探测性，使用了由引力波和X射线观测以及中子皮厚度测量最新进展所约束的现实状态方程后验。基于近期包含体粘滞的双中子星模拟，采用线性拟合公式来估计体粘滞效应引起的后并合引力波峰值频率移动。随后，我们评估了我们一组状态方程后验样本的峰值频率移动。我们使用Fisher信息矩阵和ET-CE探测器网络在乐观情景下的模拟可观测事件数据集来估计峰值频率的测量精度。我们发现，仅对于对称能量斜率（$L_{\rm sym}$）较大的状态方程模型（如中子皮厚度测量所青睐的），体粘滞效应对后并合引力波的影响才可能被探测到。因此，体粘滞效应对后并合引力波影响的探测本身可以提供关于对称能量及其斜率的丰富信息。然而，体粘滞效应对后并合引力波的影响通常较弱，并且可能仅在一些极端和乐观的情况下被观测到。因此，后并合引力波的观测及其分析可能无法通过体粘滞效应提供非常精确的对称能量测量。

## Abstract
We investigate bulk viscosity (BV) effects on post-merger gravitational wave (PMGW) signals from binary neutron star (BNS) mergers and their detectability using realistic equation of state (EoS) posteriors constrained by recent progress in gravitational-wave (GW) and X-ray observations, as well as neutron skin thickness measurements. A linear fitting formula based on recent BNS simulations with BV is used to estimate the peak frequency shift of the PMGWs caused by BV effects. Subsequently, we evaluate this peak frequency shift for our set of EoS posterior samples. We use the Fisher information matrix and the dataset of simulated observable events from the ET and CE detector network in an optimistic scenario to estimate the measurement accuracy of the peak frequency. We find that BV effects on PMGWs may be detectable only for EoS models with large values of the symmetry energy slope ($L_{\rm sym}$), as favored by neutron-skin thickness measurements. Thus, the detection of BV effects on PMGWs itself can provide abundant information about the symmetry energy and its slope. However, BV effects on PMGWs are generally weak and may be observed only in some extreme and optimistic cases. Therefore, observations of PMGWs and their analyses may not be able to provide very accurate measurements of the symmetry energy through BV effects.
