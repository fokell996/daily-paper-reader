## 摘要
我们展示了一项受控的二维研究，研究了核物质状态方程（EOS）对磁化中子星壳层爆发后热弛豫的影响。在相同的磁场和加热设定下，以固定引力质量$1.4 M_{\odot}$演化六个EOS模型，这些模型具有各EOS特有的TOV背景、壳层成分和输运输入。我们的分析结合了自洽的多EOS演化、BSk族分解（该分解将结构、微观物理和相互作用贡献分离），以及接受步长能量记录和数值灵敏度预算。模型显示了早期响应及稍后表面、壳层和内边界之间热量再分配中的EOS相关变化；在约$10^2$天附近出现的光变曲线交叉表明EOS效应并非简单的光度重新标度。到1000天时，累积表面光子能量分数相差不足一个百分点，而内部能量分配差异则大得多。更新的灵敏度测试表明，晚期光度差异和最大峰值对比超过了相应的BSk24数值灵敏度尺度，而最小的早期峰值对比仍未能被足够可靠地分辨。这些计算旨在作为可复现的EOS灵敏度基准，而非观测拟合。

## Abstract
We present a controlled two-dimensional study of nuclear-equation-of-state (EOS) effects on the post-outburst thermal relaxation of magnetized neutron-star crusts. Six EOS models are evolved at a fixed gravitational mass of $1.4 M_{\odot}$ with EOS-specific TOV backgrounds, crust compositions, and transport inputs under identical magnetic-field and heating prescriptions. Our analysis combines self-consistent multi-EOS evolution with a BSk-family factorization that separates structural, microphysical, and interaction contributions, together with an accepted-step energy ledger and a numerical-sensitivity budget. The models show EOS-dependent changes in both the early response and the later redistribution of heat between the surface, crust, and inner boundary; light-curve crossings near $10^2$ days demonstrate that the EOS effect is not a simple luminosity rescaling. By 1000 days the cumulative surface-photon energy fraction differs by less than one percentage point, whereas the internal energy partition differs much more strongly. The updated sensitivity tests show that the late-time luminosity differences and the largest peak contrasts exceed the corresponding BSk24 numerical-sensitivity scales, while the smallest early peak contrast remains less securely resolved. The calculations are intended as a reproducible EOS-sensitivity benchmark rather than an observational fit.
