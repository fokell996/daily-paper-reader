## 摘要
在极端密度下，强相互作用物质的性质和行为不仅可以通过地面实验或理论考量来确定，对中子星（NSs）和双中子星系统的天体物理观测也日益重要，以获取互补信息。鉴于这些发现，我们开展了一项贝叶斯研究，通过纳入直接 Urca（dUrca）过程的约束来推断中子星状态方程（EoS），从而在传统使用的宏观可观测量之外，还纳入物质组成信息。我们在 JESTER 框架中实现了对 β 平衡和电荷中性的自洽处理，包括计算质子分数以及存在电子和 μ 子时核子 dUrca 的起始。我们结合了来自手征有效场论的约束、对中子星质量、半径和潮汐形变度的天体物理测量，以及对快速冷却和缓慢冷却中子星的观测。dUrca 约束似乎倾向于更硬的 EoS，但——在当前阶段——一旦纳入其他核物理与天体物理约束，它对宏观中子星性质只有较小影响。相反，dUrca 信息对典型质量中子星的组成提供了约束，倾向于星体内部具有更高的质子分数，这说明了冷却观测所携带的互补信息。我们的结果展示了将组成敏感的可观测量纳入多信使推断的潜力，并朝着利用具有微观动机的 EoS 模型更全面地处理 dUrca 约束迈出了一步。

## Abstract
The properties and behavior of strongly interacting matter at extreme densities can not only be determined through terrestrial experiments or through theoretical considerations, but also astrophysical observations of neutron stars (NSs) and binary neutron star systems become increasingly important to obtain complementary information. In light of these findings, we perform a Bayesian study inferring the NS equation of state (EoS) by incorporating constraints from the direct Urca (dUrca) process, thereby including information on matter composition in addition to traditionally used macroscopic observables. We implement a self-consistent treatment of $β$-equilibrium and charge neutrality in the \textsc{jester} framework, including the calculation of the proton fraction and the onset of nucleonic dUrca in the presence of electrons and muons. We combine constraints from chiral effective field theory, astrophysical measurements of NS masses, radii, and tidal deformabilities, and observations of rapidly and slowly cooling neutron stars. The dUrca constraint seems to be in favor of stiffer EoSs, but --at the current stage-- has only a minor impact on macroscopic NS properties once we include other nuclear and astrophysical constraints. In contrast, the dUrca information provides a constraint on the composition of canonical-mass NSs favoring a higher proton fraction inside the star, illustrating the complementary information carried by cooling observations. Our results demonstrate the potential of incorporating composition-sensitive observables into multimessenger inference and provide a step toward a more comprehensive treatment of dUrca constraints with microscopically motivated EoS models.
