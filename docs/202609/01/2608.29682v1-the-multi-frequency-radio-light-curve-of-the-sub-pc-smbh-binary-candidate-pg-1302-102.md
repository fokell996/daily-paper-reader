## 摘要
具有亚秒差距（sub-pc）轨道间距的超大质量黑洞双星（SMBHB）候选体是罕见且高价值的系统，被认为是近期探测到的纳赫兹（nHz）引力波（GW）背景的主要源。除了利用脉冲星计时阵搜索来自SMBHB的局域化纳赫兹引力波源之外，通过长时间基线的大规模类星体光学光变曲线巡天，已识别出越来越多的候选体。红移$z=0.278$的类星体PG~1302-102的光学光变曲线在约20年的时间里表现出一个显著的、平滑变化的约5.2年周期信号。尽管这一周期性的成因仍有争议，一个合理的解释是两颗亚秒差距间距的超大质量黑洞组成的双星系统。在此近距离下，PG 1302-102中$10^{8.3-9.4} M_{\odot}$的核区黑洞预计仅因引力波辐射就会在约$10^{5}$年内并合。我们展示了PG~1302-102跨越约19年基线的多频射电光变曲线分析，观测数据来自澳大利亚望远镜致密阵（ATCA），时间为2005年至2024年。ATCA射电光变曲线在5.5~GHz处高度可变，但没有显示出光学波段所见周期性的显著证据。然而，我们报告在5.5~GHz波段内谱指数时间序列中存在略微更强的周期性证据，其周期与光学周期性一致。此外，最近报道的光学周期性变化也出现在射电波段内谱指数光变曲线中。这两个结果表明，尽管射电和光学发射机制截然不同，但它们的周期性可能物理耦合。我们认为，这种射电-光学耦合强化了SMBHB的解读，尽管存在光学周期性变化，并在LSST-SKAO时代之前提供了一个有趣的案例研究。

## Abstract
Supermassive black hole binary (SMBHB) candidates with sub-parsec (sub-pc) orbital separations are rare, high-value systems, thought to be the dominant sources of the recently detected nanoHertz (nHz) gravitational wave (GW) background. Alongside pulsar-timing-array searches for localized nHz GW sources from SMBHBs, an increasing number of candidates have been identified from synoptic surveys of large quasar optical light curves over increasingly long baselines. The optical light curve of the quasar PG~1302-102 at a redshift of $z=0.278$ exhibits a pronounced, smoothly varying periodic signal of ~5.2 years over approximately 20 years. Although the cause of this periodicity remains debated, a plausible explanation is a binary system of two SMBHs with a sub-pc separation. At this close distance, the $10^{8.3-9.4} M_{\odot}$ nuclear black holes in PG 1302-102 are expected to merge within $\sim$$10^{5}$ yr solely due to GW emission. We present a multi-frequency radio light curve analysis of PG~1302-102 spanning a $\sim$19-year baseline, with observations between 2005 and 2024 drawn from the Australian Telescope Compact Array (ATCA). The ATCA radio light curve is highly variable at 5.5~GHz but shows no significant evidence of the periodicity seen at optical wavelengths. However, we report fractionally stronger evidence of periodicity in the in-band spectral index time-series at 5.5~GHz, with a period consistent with the optical periodicity. Furthermore, the recently reported change in optical periodicity is also present in the radio in-band spectral index light curve. These two results suggest that radio and optical periodicity may be physically coupled despite very different emission mechanisms. We argue that this radio-optical coupling reinforces the SMBHB interpretation, despite the optical periodicity change, and provides an intriguing case study ahead of the LSST-SKAO era.
