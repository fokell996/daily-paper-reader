## 摘要
脉冲星计时阵列（PTAs）已探测到纳赫兹引力波背景，常被归因于湮灭的畴壁（DWs）或一阶相变（FOPTs）。它们的再加热会在重子光子比中留下涨落。这些所谓的重子等曲率涨落 $S_B$ 一直存留到大爆炸核合成时期，而后者的产额非线性依赖于它。因此，适度的 $S_B$ 涨落可以在 PTA 偏好区域的某些部分缓解当前氘张力，从而加强其解释。过多的 DWs 和过强的 FOPTs 会过量产生 $D/H$，因而被排除。

## Abstract
Pulsar timing arrays (PTAs) have detected a nanohertz gravitational-wave background, often attributed to annihilating domain walls (DWs) or first-order phase transitions (FOPTs). Their reheating imprints fluctuations in the baryon-to-photon ratio. These so-called baryon isocurvature fluctuations $S_B$ survive until Big Bang Nucleosynthesis, whose yields depend nonlinearly on it. Modest $S_B$ fluctuations can therefore alleviate the current deuterium tension in parts of the PTA preferred regions and therefore strengthen their interpretation. Overabundant DWs and too strong FOPTs overproduce $D/H$ and are ruled out.
