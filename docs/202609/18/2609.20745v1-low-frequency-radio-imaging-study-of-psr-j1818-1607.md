## 摘要
我们报告了利用升级后的巨型米波射电望远镜在band 3（300–500 MHz）和band 4（550–750 MHz）对磁星PSR J1818−1607进行的低频射电观测。我们识别出与该磁星相关的连续谱源，并报告了通量密度及其谱指数的变化。该磁星的通量密度时间序列揭示了两次可能具有不同谱特性的射电耀发事件。我们讨论了谱指数变化对射电强磁星潜在辐射机制的影响。我们还报告了在多个档案射电成像巡天中未探测到该连续谱源，也没有直接迹象表明存在相关的弥散辐射。

## Abstract
We report on the low-frequency radio observations of magnetar PSR J1818$-$1607 carried out with the upgraded Giant Metrewave Radio Telescope at band 3 (300$-$500 MHz) and band 4 (550-750 MHz). We have identified the continuum source associated with the magnetar and report variations in flux density and its spectral index. The flux density timeseries for the magnetar reveals the presence of two potential radio flaring episodes with varying spectral properties. We discuss the implications of the spectral index changes on the potential emission mechanisms for radio-loud magnetars. We also report non-detections of the continuum source as well as no direct indication for an associated diffuse emission from multiple archival radio imaging surveys.

- **研究背景**：磁星是年轻、强磁场中子星，具有高磁场（\(10^{14}-10^{15}\) G）、长自转周期和高自转减慢率。银河系中已确认/候选磁星约 30 颗，但仅少数表现为“射电噪”并具有射电脉冲辐射。
