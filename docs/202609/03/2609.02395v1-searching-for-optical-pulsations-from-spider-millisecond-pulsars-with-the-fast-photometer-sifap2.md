## 摘要
最近，从一颗过渡毫秒脉冲星和一颗吸积毫秒脉冲星中发现了光学脉冲辐射。对于旋转动力的红背毫秒脉冲星，也发现了光学脉冲辐射的证据。观测表明，光学脉冲辐射的产生机制取决于系统中是否存在吸积盘。我们研究了一批处于紧密双星系统中的旋转动力毫秒脉冲星（轨道周期P$_{orb}<1$天，红背和黑寡妇系统），使用安装在伽利略国家望远镜（TNG）上的SiFAP2光度计搜索光学周期信号。我们利用每个系统最新的（射电或伽马射线）轨道解，采用折叠历元方法进行脉冲搜索，这些轨道解来自已发表的解决方案或最近在楠赛、巨型米波射电望远镜、帕克斯天文台和费米- LAT的观测。我们更新或确认了其中五个系统的射电和伽马射线星历表。尽管我们经常达到接近仪器极限的灵敏度，但在所有搜索中均未检测到超过3σ置信水平的光学脉冲信号。这为系统的光学脉冲星等和转换效率设置了相应的严格上限。我们的上限与红背脉冲星PSR J2339-0533的光学脉冲光度一致。与吸积和过渡毫秒脉冲星相比，红背和黑寡妇系统在将自转减速功率转换为光学脉冲辐射方面的效率要低得多，这支持了最近提出的观点，即中子星周围的吸积盘在加速产生明亮光学脉冲辐射的粒子方面发挥作用。

## Abstract
Optical pulsations from millisecond pulsars (MSPs) have recently been discovered from a transitional MSP and an accreting MSP. Evidence for optical pulsations has also been found for a rotation-powered redback MSP. Observations suggest that optical pulsed emission is produced by different mechanisms depending on whether an accretion disk is present in the system or not. We explore a sample of rotation-powered MSPs in close binaries (P$_{orb}<1\,$day, redbacks and black widows), searching for optical periodic signals with the $SiFAP2$ photometer mounted on the $Telescopio\ Nazionale\ Galileo$ (TNG). We perform pulsation searches using the epoch folding method with the most updated (radio or gamma-ray) orbital solution for each system, employing already published solutions or recent observations with Nançay, the Giant Metrewave Radio Telescope, the Parkes Observatory, and $Fermi$-LAT. We update or confirm radio and gamma-ray ephemerides for five of the presented systems. No optical pulsations were detected with a significance exceeding a $3σ$ confidence level in any of our searches, despite often achieving sensitivities on the verge of the instrument's limit. This places correspondingly stringent upper limits on the systems' optical pulsed magnitude and conversion efficiency. Our upper limits are consistent with the pulsed optical luminosity of optical pulsations from the redback PSR J2339-0533. Compared to accreting and transitional MSPs, redback and black widow systems show much lower efficiency in converting their spin down power into pulsed optical emission, supporting the recently advanced idea that the accretion disk around the neutron star plays a role in accelerating the particles responsible for producing bright optical pulsed emission.
