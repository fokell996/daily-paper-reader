## 摘要
我们展示了对两颗年老毫秒脉冲星、一颗年轻高能脉冲星和一颗年轻中子星的新获取及存档的Chandra和NuSTAR数据的分析。PSR J1810-0623是一颗新发现的4.55毫秒射电脉冲星，位于双星系统中，其伴星可能是一颗碳氧白矮星。我们利用一次新的短期Chandra HRC-I观测，首次在X射线波段初步探测到该脉冲星。其微弱程度表明其距离更接近色散测量推断距离中较远的那个（约1千秒差距）。PSR J1933-6211是一颗3.54毫秒射电脉冲星，同样具有碳氧白矮星伴星。我们在存档HRC-S图像中首次对其进行了X射线探测，表明其X射线光度与1.9倍太阳质量的脉冲星PSR J1614-2230相似。接下来，PSR J1813-1749是一颗年龄为1-2千年的脉冲星，具有已知最高的自转减慢光度之一。我们利用2025年NuSTAR和2026年Chandra ACIS-S数据测量了其自转周期为44.7毫秒，并更新了其自转演化模型。我们首次展示了PSR J1813-1749在3-79 keV能段的脉冲轮廓，并发现脉冲星及其脉冲星风云在该能段的谱在2018年和2025年的观测之间没有显著变化。最后，对于超新星遗迹G18.95-1.1中年龄为5-10千年的中子星CXOU J182913.1-125113，我们合并了2009年和2024年的ACIS-I观测，以更好地测量该中子星的能谱。

## Abstract
We present analyses of new and archival Chandra and NuSTAR data on two old millisecond pulsars, a young highly-energetic pulsar, and a young neutron star. PSR J1810-0623 is a recently-discovered 4.55 ms radio pulsar in a binary with a possible carbon-oxygen white dwarf companion. We tentatively detect the pulsar for the first time in X-ray using a new short Chandra HRC-I observation. Its faintness suggests a distance more in accord with the further (~1 kpc) of its dispersion measure-inferred distances. PSR J1933-6211 is a 3.54 ms radio pulsar also with a carbon-oxygen white dwarf companion. Our first-time X-ray detection in an archival HRC-S image indicates its X-ray luminosity is similar to that of the 1.9 Msun pulsar PSR J1614-2230. Next, PSR J1813-1749 is a 1-2 kyr pulsar with one of the highest known spin-down luminosities. We measure its spin period of 44.7 ms using 2025 NuSTAR and 2026 Chandra ACIS-S data and update its spin evolution model. We show for the first time the pulse profile of PSR J1813-1749 at 3-79 keV, and we find that the spectra of the pulsar and pulsar wind nebula at these energies did not change significantly between observations in 2018 and 2025. Finally, for the 5-10 kyr neutron star CXOU J182913.1-125113 in the supernova remnant G18.95-1.1, we combine ACIS-I observations from 2009 and 2024 to better measure the neutron star's spectrum.
