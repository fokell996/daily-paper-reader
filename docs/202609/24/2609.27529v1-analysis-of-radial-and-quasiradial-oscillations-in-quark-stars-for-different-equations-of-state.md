## 摘要
我们研究了由三种夸克物质（QM）状态方程（EoSs）描述的夸克星（QSs），并在慢旋转相对论近似下分析了它们的径向与准径向振荡模式。所考虑的状态方程分别描述无质量夸克、有质量夸克，以及具有温度依赖的胶子质量的有质量夸克。我们对径向模式的分析表明，模式间隔依赖于给定致密性下的星体质量与半径，也依赖于星体温度。所考虑的QS模型满足Hartle、Thorne和Chitre形式体系的慢旋转相对论条件，从而可将旋转视为对非旋转位形的小扰动。我们发现，尽管质量更大且尺度更大的星体可以表现出更大的表面偏心率和四极形变，但其更强的自引力导致其形变倾向低于质量较小且尺度较小的星体。对于所有模型，质量分布的变化向星体表面方向增大，而压强、能量密度和数密度的变化在中心附近仍然很小，并向表面方向变得更加显著。更致密的星体表现出更强的局部惯性系扭曲，但在给定角速度下表现出更弱的参考系拖曳。旋转使振荡模式偏离其非旋转值，并在不同角速度下导致不同的振动衰减时间。这里考虑的基本模式频率低于 $2$ kHz，并在所研究的各种旋转位形中表现出不连续行为。

## Abstract
We investigate quark stars (QSs) described by three quark matter (QM)equations of state (EoSs) and analyse their radial and quasiradial oscillation modes within the slowly rotating relativistic approximation. The considered EoSs describe massless quarks, massive quarks, and massive quarks with a temperature-dependent gluon mass. Our analysis of radial modes shows that the mode separation depends on the stellar mass and radius for a given compactness, as well as on the stellar temperature. The considered QS models satisfy the slowly rotating relativistic condition of Hartle, Thorne and Chitre formalism, allowing rotation to be treated as a small perturbation of the non-rotating configuration. We find that, although more massive and larger stars can exhibit greater surface eccentricity and quadrupole deformation, their stronger self-gravity results in a lower tendency to deform than less massive and smaller stars. For all models, the variation in mass distribution increases towards the stellar surface, while the variations in pressure, energy density, and number density remain small near the center and become more pronounced towards the surface. More compact stars exhibit stronger twisting of the local inertial frame but weaker frame dragging for a given angular velocity. Rotation shifts the oscillation modes from their non-rotating values and leads to different vibrational decay times at different angular velocities. The fundamental modes considered here exhibit frequencies below $2$ kHz and show non-continuous behaviour across the investigated rotational configurations.
