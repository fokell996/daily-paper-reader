## 摘要
大质量恒星坍缩中形成的快速旋转磁星，可通过准直极向喷流和准各向同性磁星风释放其自转减速功率。凭借其持久的软 X 射线黑体成分以及 SN 2006aj 的双峰紫外/光学光变曲线，GRB 060218-SN 2006aj 为这种双成分能量注入情景提供了一个有用的检验案例。我们建立了一个磁星驱动的喷流-星风模型，其中两种外流与同源膨胀的超新星抛射物相互作用，驱动前向激波，产生激波突破辐射以及随后的磁星驱动热辐射。我们首先使用喷流成分拟合 GRB 060218 的 0.3–10 keV 黑体通量和温度演化，随后加入星风成分来模拟 SN 2006aj 的紫外/光学光变曲线。我们发现，软 X 射线黑体成分主要可由喷流驱动的极向抛射物产生的激波突破辐射再现。双峰紫外/光学光变曲线则是由准各向同性磁星风与一个质量更大的抛射物成分之间的相互作用产生的。第一个光学峰由星风驱动的激波突破辐射主导，而更宽且更晚的辐射则主要由随后的磁星驱动热成分供能。我们的结果表明，GRB 060218-SN 2006aj 可用一个双成分磁星引擎图景来描述，其中 X 射线热辐射和紫外/光学双峰演化源自同一中心引擎的不同能量注入通道。

## Abstract
A rapidly rotating magnetar formed in the collapse of a massive star may release its spin-down power through both a collimated polar jet and a quasi-isotropic magnetar wind. With its long-lasting soft X-ray blackbody component and the double-peaked UV/optical light curves of SN 2006aj, GRB 060218-SN 2006aj provides a useful test case for such a two-component energy-injection scenario. We developed a magnetar-powered jet-wind model in which the two outflows interact with homologously expanding SN ejecta, driving forward shocks that produce shock-breakout emission and subsequent magnetar-powered thermal emission. We first used the jet component to fit the 0.3-10 keV blackbody flux and temperature evolution of GRB 060218 and subsequently added the wind component to model the UV/optical light curves of SN 2006aj. We find that the soft X-ray blackbody component can be reproduced mainly by shock-breakout emission from the polar ejecta driven by the jet. The double-peaked UV/optical light curves are instead produced by the interaction between the quasi-isotropic magnetar wind and a more massive ejecta component. The first optical peak is dominated by wind-driven shock-breakout emission, whereas the broader and later emission is powered mainly by the subsequent magnetar-powered thermal component. Our results suggest a two-component magnetar-engine picture for GRB 060218-SN 2006aj, in which the X-ray thermal emission and the UV/optical double-peaked evolution arise from different energy-injection channels of the same central engine.
