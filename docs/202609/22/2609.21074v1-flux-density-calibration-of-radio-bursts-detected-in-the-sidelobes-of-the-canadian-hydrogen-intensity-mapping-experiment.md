## 摘要
加拿大氢强度测绘实验/快速射电暴（CHIME/FRB）仪器是一个瞬变天体物理暴巡天项目，正在发现快速射电暴、脉冲星和长周期瞬变源。作为一台中天望远镜，该巡天在子午线±2度范围内最灵敏；然而，CHIME/FRB 能够在其地平线范围内探测到明亮的天体物理暴，其灵敏度仅为峰值灵敏度的 10^-2 到 10^-3。在本研究中，我们详细介绍了对在 CHIME 旁瓣中探测到的这些暴的流量密度校准流程，涵盖通道化电压（基带）和强度数据。我们通过将基带数据波束形成指向旁瓣中的明亮稳定源、比较同一批天体物理瞬变源的基带与强度流量密度校准，以及对过中天的稳定源进行强度流量密度校准，来验证并估计校准不确定度。我们能够对赤纬从 -12 度到 +59 度、时角达到 30 度的源进行流量密度校准，并且对于赤纬接近金牛座 A（蟹状星云，赤纬约 +22 度）的源，校准时角可达到 90 度。我们得出结论：对于 CHIME 旁瓣中的瞬变源，流量密度校准的不确定度范围为 20% 至 42%，具体取决于所用数据和该暴的时角。

## Abstract
The Canadian Hydrogen Intensity Mapping Experiment/Fast Radio Burst (CHIME/FRB) instrument is a transient astrophysical burst survey that is discovering FRBs, pulsars, and long-period transients. As a transit telescope, the survey is most sensitive within 2 degrees of the meridian; however, CHIME/FRB can detect bright astrophysical bursts out to its horizon, at 10^-2 to 10^-3 of its peak sensitivity. In this study, we detail the flux density calibration procedure for these bursts detected in the CHIME sidelobes for both channelized voltage (baseband) and intensity data. We validate and estimate the calibration uncertainties by beamforming baseband data towards bright, steady sources in the sidelobes, comparing baseband and intensity flux density calibrations of the same astrophysical transients, and calibrating intensity flux densities for transiting steady sources. We are able to calibrate flux densities for declinations spanning -12 degrees to +59 degrees out to hour angles of 30 degrees, and out to 90 degrees in hour angle for declinations near that of Taurus A (Crab Nebula, declination of about +22 degrees). We conclude that uncertainties in the flux density calibration for transients in the CHIME sidelobes range from 20% to 42%, depending on the data used and the hour angle of the burst.
