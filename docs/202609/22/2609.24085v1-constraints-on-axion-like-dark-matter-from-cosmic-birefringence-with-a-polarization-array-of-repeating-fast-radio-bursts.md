## 摘要
类轴子暗物质（ALDM）是一种动机充分的暗物质候选者，其与光子的 Chern-Simons 耦合可以诱导宇宙双折射，从而在不同源的偏振角（PA）信号中产生时间振荡和空间关联。具有高线偏振的超活跃重复快速射电暴（FRB）是探测这类特征特别有前景的探针。我们开发了一个贝叶斯框架，用于在单个重复 FRB 中搜寻由 ALDM 引起的 PA 振荡，并通过利用共同地球项产生的关联，将多个源组合成一个 FRB 偏振阵列（FRBPA）。我们将该框架应用于三个研究充分的超活跃重复 FRB 的 PA 数据集，并在广泛的 ALDM 质量范围内限制 Chern-Simons 耦合。对于每个 FRB，我们使用经模拟校准的暗物质晕轮廓估计其宿主位置的暗物质密度。推断出的暗物质密度与基准局域密度 $\rho_0 = 0.4~\mathrm{GeV/cm^3}$ 相当。源和地球暗物质密度相当，加之红移导致的源项与地球项频率差异，促使我们采用完整的两分量双折射模型。在单个源分析或联合 FRBPA 分析中，我们都没有发现 ALDM 诱导的 PA 振荡的有力证据。我们将 Chern-Simons 耦合强度作为 ALDM 质量的函数，给出 95% 可信上限。在轴子质量从 $10^{-23}$ 到 $10^{-18}~\mathrm{eV}$ 的范围内，FRBPA 得到的耦合强度上限介于 $10^{-12.6}$ 和 $10^{-7.9}~\mathrm{GeV}^{-1}$ 之间。我们的结果确立了长期偏振监测超活跃重复 FRB 作为 ALDM 的一种有前景且独立的探针，并预期其灵敏度将随更长的观测基线和更大的源样本而提高。

## Abstract
Axion-like dark matter (ALDM) is a well-motivated dark matter candidate whose Chern-Simons coupling to photons can induce cosmic birefringence, producing temporal oscillations and spatial correlation in polarization angle (PA) signals from different sources. Hyperactive repeating fast radio bursts (FRBs) with high linear polarization are a particularly promising probe of such signatures. We develop a Bayesian framework to search for PA oscillations caused by ALDM in individual repeating FRBs, and combine multiple sources in an FRB polarization array (FRBPA) by exploiting correlations arising from the common Earth term. We apply this framework to PA datasets from three well-studied hyperactive repeating FRBs, and constrain the Chern-Simons coupling over a broad range of ALDM masses. For each FRB, we estimate the dark matter density at its host location using a simulation-calibrated halo profile. The inferred dark matter densities are comparable to the fiducial local density $ρ_0 = 0.4~\mathrm{GeV/cm^3}$. The comparable source and Earth dark matter densities, together with the redshift-induced difference between the frequencies of the source and Earth terms, motivate our adoption of the full two-component birefringence model. We find no compelling evidence for ALDM-induced PA oscillations in either the individual source analyses or the joint FRBPA analysis. We place 95\% credible upper limits on the Chern-Simons coupling strength as a function of ALDM mass. Across the axion mass from $10^{-23}$ to $10^{-18}~\mathrm{eV}$, the resulting upper limits of the coupling strength of FRBPA lie between $10^{-12.6}$ and $10^{-7.9}~\mathrm{GeV}^{-1}$. Our results establish long-term polarimetric monitoring of hyperactive repeating FRBs as a promising and independent probe of ALDM, with sensitivity expected to improve with longer observational baselines and larger source samples.
