## 摘要
4U 0114+65 是一个由 B1 Ia 超巨星 V* V662 Cas 和一颗已知最慢的吸积中子星组成的高质量 X 射线双星，其自旋周期约为 9.4 ks。2025 年，其长周期 X 射线脉冲在 Swift/BAT 监测中变得无法探测，这促使我们利用 XMM-Newton 进行了主任自由裁量时间观测。我们将该观测与 2015 年 XMM-Newton 观测进行比较，当时源更亮且脉冲清晰，并利用 Swift/BAT 数据分析其长期自旋演化。我们采用与以往工作相同的模型进行了平均光谱和脉冲相位分辨光谱分析。2025 年的观测仍显示出弱脉冲，周期约为 9.3 ks，尽管它们在 Swift/BAT 中未被探测到。两个历元中的整体光谱形状保持相似，但光度下降了约一个数量级，主要是由于体运动康普顿化分量被强烈抑制。尽管 2025 年的吸收柱密度更高，推断出的星风性质仍与 2015 年大致相符，表明供体星风无需发生重大全局变化。相反，结果表明中子星磁层附近的吸积效率大幅降低。我们提出，4U 0114+65 可能正在向强耦合区中的部分离心抑制演化，其中环向磁场分量与极向磁场分量相当。如果得到证实，这将是该状态的首个观测证据。吸积将逐渐变得效率更低且更间歇，而不会达到完全发展的螺旋桨机制。长期硬 X 射线监测中脉冲的表观消失，将是光度降低和绝对脉冲通量减小所致，而不是由于潜在自旋调制的丧失。

## Abstract
4U~0114+65 is a high-mass X-ray binary composed of the B1\,Ia supergiant V*~V662~Cas and one of the slowest known accreting neutron stars, with a spin period of $\sim$9.4 ks. In 2025, its long X-ray pulsations became undetectable in \textit{Swift}/BAT monitoring, motivating a Director's Discretionary Time observation with \textit{XMM-Newton}. We compare this observation with a 2015 \textit{XMM-Newton} observation, when the source was brighter and clearly pulsed, and analyze the long-term spin evolution using \textit{Swift}/BAT data. We performed average and pulse-phase-resolved spectroscopy using the same model as in previous work. The 2025 observation still reveals weak pulsations, with a period of about 9.3 ks, despite their non-detection in \textit{Swift}/BAT. The overall spectral shape remains similar in both epochs, but the luminosity decreased by about one order of magnitude, mainly due to strong suppression of the bulk-motion Comptonization component. Although the absorbing column is higher in 2025, the inferred wind properties remain broadly compatible with those from 2015, suggesting that no major global change in the donor wind is required. Instead, the results point to a substantial reduction in accretion efficiency close to the neutron-star magnetosphere. We propose that 4U~0114+65 may be evolving toward partial centrifugal inhibition in the strong-coupling regime, where the toroidal magnetic-field component is comparable to the poloidal one. If confirmed, this would represent the first observational evidence of this state. Accretion would become progressively less efficient and more intermittent without reaching a fully developed propeller regime. The apparent disappearance of the pulse in long-term hard X-ray monitoring would then result from the lower luminosity and reduced absolute pulsed flux, rather than from the loss of the underlying spin modulation.
