## 摘要
脉冲星磁层中等离子体的运动基本上沿弯曲磁力线进行，这可能导致辐射束扫过观测者的视线。我们表明，在某些条件下，这种经过的束元可以仅通过对辐射贡献的平均产生两个正交偏振模式（OPM），而不涉及双折射。这要求来自通过过程不同阶段的辐射以非相干方式贡献，因此相干时间尺度远短于典型采样时间。该模型产生两个强度相当的OPM，并具有宽范围的（单脉冲）偏振分数，这与一般观测到的脉冲星性质一致。在所需特定条件下（本质上是在适当时间尺度上对信号进行非相干求和），仅平均效应便可导致出现具有OPM跳变的两条正交偏振轨迹。

## Abstract
Motion of plasma in pulsar magnetospheres essentially follows bent magnetic field lines which may result in radiation beams sweeping past the observer's sightline. We show that under certain conditions such a passing beamlet can produce two orthogonal polarization modes (OPMs) solely from averaging of radiative contributions, without involving birefringence. This requires that radiation from different stages of the passage is contributed incoherently, hence that the timescale for the coherence is much shorter than the typical sampling time. The model produces two OPMs of comparable amount, with a wide range of (single-pulse) polarized fractions, which is consistent with general observed pulsar properties. Under the specific conditions required (essentially incoherent summation of signal at appropriate time scale) the averaging effects alone can thus lead to the appearance of two orthogonal polarization tracks with OPM jumps.
