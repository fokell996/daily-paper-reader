## 摘要
我们报告在MeerKAT和ASKAP巡天数据中偶然发现J155543.7−563102（以下简称LPT J1555−5631），其周期为62.2分钟。该源在2022年5月一个15小时的活动窗口内发射了明亮（10–30 mJy）、陡谱（α ∼ −1.5）、高度偏振的射电脉冲，而在约5年前后均未探测到其他宁静或脉冲发射。平均光变曲线显示每周期有两个交替脉冲。主脉冲（MP）和中间脉冲（IP）相隔Δφ=165.4°，振幅相似，但偏振性质不同：较宽的MP（3.9%）具有部分线偏振和圆偏振，并显示恒定的偏振位置角，而较窄的IP（1.1%）为约100%圆偏振。我们将LPT J1555−5631归类为近正交转子，即在一次自转中可看到来自两个磁极的辐射，且磁轴与自转轴几乎垂直。与对跖分离的14.6°偏差以及几乎恒定的偏振位置角表明，该辐射并非来自普通脉冲星的标准偶极极冠几何，而是起源于更宽广、更复杂的磁层。LPT J1555−5631的观测性质可由延展的辐射区域容纳，并支持相干辐射机制，如电子回旋脉泽不稳定性，尽管它们并不能唯一确定其前身星或辐射物理。如果这些推论更广泛地适用于LPT群体，它们可以解释为何其波束宽度与自转周期解耦、平坦偏振位置角普遍存在，并可能支持正交转子出现率升高。

## Abstract
We report the serendipitous discovery of J155543.7$-$563102 (hereafter LPT J1555$-$5631) with a period of 62.2 min in MeerKAT and ASKAP survey data. The source emitted bright (10-30 mJy), steep-spectrum ($α\sim-1.5$), highly polarized radio pulses during a 15-hr active window in May 2022, with no other quiescent or pulsed emission detected before or after over $\sim5$ years. The mean light curve exhibits two alternating pulses per cycle. A main pulse (MP) and an interpulse (IP) separated by $Δφ=165.4^\circ$ with similar amplitudes, but different polarization properties: the wider MP (3.9\%) is partially linearly and circularly polarized and shows a constant polarization position angle, while the narrow IP (1.1\%) is $\sim$100\% circularly polarized. We classify LPT J1555$-$5631 as a near-orthogonal rotator, for which emission from both magnetic poles is visible during one rotation and the magnetic and rotation axes are almost perpendicular. The 14.6$^\circ$ deviation from an antipodal separation and the nearly constant polarization position angle suggest that the emission does not arise from the standard dipolar polar-cap geometry of ordinary pulsars, but instead originates within a broader, more complex magnetosphere. The observed properties of LPT J1555$-$5631 can be accommodated by extended emission regions, and favor coherent emission mechanisms such as the electron cyclotron maser instability, although they do not uniquely determine the progenitor or emission physics. If these inferences apply more broadly to the LPT population, they could account for why their beam widths are decoupled from rotation periods, the prevalence of flat polarization position angles, and may favor an elevated incidence of orthogonal rotators.
