## 摘要
脉冲星色散量（DM）的时间变化可探测电离星际介质（IISM）中自由电子密度的涨落。尽管毫秒脉冲星的DM变化已被广泛研究，但年轻脉冲星提供了互补的探针，因为其视线（LoS）穿过超新星遗迹、脉冲星风星云以及其他湍流环境。然而，针对年轻脉冲星DM变化的系统性研究仍然有限。本文中，我们刻画了大样本年轻脉冲星的DM时间变化，以约束沿其视线的IISM湍流，并探究所观测变化的物理起源。我们分析了由Murriyang/Parkes射电望远镜获得的364颗年轻脉冲星18年的计时观测，将21厘米多波束接收机的遗留数据与超宽带低频（UWL）接收机的近期观测相结合。逐历元的DM测量由子带到达时间导出，并利用线性趋势和DM结构函数来表征其变化，随后与Kolmogorov湍流的预期进行比较。在UWL数据中探测到89颗脉冲星具有显著DM变化，在多波束数据中探测到23颗，合并数据集中有16颗显示出显著趋势。这代表了脉冲星DM变化方面最大规模的长时间基线研究之一。我们发现初步证据表明，观测到的DM变化主要与Kolmogorov湍流一致。脉冲星J1105-6107和J1829-1751显示出由周期性轮廓变化驱动的估计DM变化。我们还识别出21颗明亮的慢速脉冲星，其短期DM变化超出了IISM的预期。

## Abstract
Temporal variations in the dispersion measure (DM) of pulsars probe fluctuations in the free-electron density of the ionized interstellar medium (IISM). While DM variations have been studied extensively in millisecond pulsars, young pulsars provide a complementary probe due to their lines of sight (LoSs) intersecting supernova remnants, pulsar wind nebulae, and other turbulent environments. However, systematic studies of DM variability in young pulsars remain limited. In this paper, We characterize the temporal DM variations of a large population of young pulsars to constrain the turbulence of the IISM along their LoSs and to investigate the physical origins of the observed variability. We analyzed 18 years of timing observations of 364 young pulsars obtained with the Murriyang/Parkes radio telescope, combining legacy data from the 21 cm Multibeam receiver with recent observations from the ultra-wideband low-frequency (UWL) receiver. Epoch-wise DM measurements were derived from sub-banded times of arrival, and the variability was characterized using linear trends and DM structure functions, which were compared with expectations for a Kolmogorov turbulence. Significant DM variations are detected in 89 pulsars in the UWL data and in 23 pulsars in the multibeam data, with 16 in the combined dataset showing significant trends. This represents one of the largest long-baseline studies of DM variability in pulsars. We find tentative evidence that the observed DM variations are predominantly consistent with Kolmogorov turbulence. PSRs J1105-6107 and J1829-1751 show estimated DM variations driven by periodic profile variability. We also identify 21 bright, slow pulsars exhibiting short-term DM variations that exceed expectations from the IISM.
