## 摘要
中子星的频率依赖潮汐响应受到因果性、实性和无源性的约束；在适当条件下，这些约束将适当重标度后的响应变为复频率上半平面的一个全纯自映射。Schwarz--Pick 定理进而给出潮汐形变率的领头动力学修正的上界。在这项工作中，我们将该上界与文献中最新的动力学潮汐响应计算进行对照；这些计算还表明，静态潮汐形变率及其在频率二阶的修正紧密相关。我们发现该上界在百分之几的范围内达到饱和，这一定量结果证实了相对论区域中 f 模的主导地位。因此，将静态形变率与对 f 模频率的独立测定相结合，或可为动力学潮汐提供一个不依赖于状态方程的上限。

## Abstract
The frequency-dependent tidal response of a neutron star is constrained by causality, reality and passivity, which turn a suitably rescaled response into a holomorphic self-map of the upper complex frequency half-plane after some suitable conditions. The Schwarz--Pick theorem then bounds the leading dynamical correction to the tidal deformability from above. In this work we confront this bound with the most recent calculations of the dynamical tidal response in the literature which have also shown that the static tidal deformability and its correction at second order in frequency are tightly correlated. We find that the bound is saturated to within a few per cent, a quantitative confirmation of f-mode dominance in the relativistic regime. The combination of the static deformability with an independent determination of the f-mode frequency thus may provide an equation-of-state-independent upper limit on the dynamical tide.
