## 摘要
高重子密度下强相互作用物质的相结构仍是 QCD 中的一个未决问题，对中子星的内部结构和磁性质具有重要意义。我们在包含张量相互作用的两味 Nambu-Jona-Lasinio 模型框架内，重新考察冷稠密夸克物质中夸克自旋极化（QSP）相的出现，重点关注其对正规化的依赖。我们比较传统正规化方案（TRS）与介质分离方案（MSS）：前者对真空和介质贡献均施加尖锐动量截断，后者则将紫外发散的真空项分离出来，而有限的、依赖介质的相空间贡献保持无截断。这两种方案导致定性不同的结果。在 TRS 中，QSP 凝聚体在化学势较高时最终坍缩，导致非单调的热力学行为，并且对于某些耦合会出现因果性破坏。在 MSS 中，QSP 凝聚体反而随密度单调增加，状态方程保持因果性，并且相关的自发磁化在高密度下增强。高密度下自旋极化的持续存在可能为致密星（包括磁星）的强磁场提供微观贡献。一项探索性的混合星构造进一步表明，QSP 相能够显著改变状态方程、稳定性趋势和质量-半径关系。这些结果凸显了一致处理介质相空间的重要性，并表明持续存在的自旋极化夸克相对中子星物理的潜在影响。

## Abstract
The phase structure of strongly interacting matter at high baryon density remains an open problem in QCD, with important implications for the internal structure and magnetic properties of neutron stars. We reexamine the emergence of a quark spin-polarized (QSP) phase in cold, dense quark matter within the two-flavor Nambu-Jona-Lasinio model with tensor interactions, focusing on its dependence on regularization. We compare the traditional regularization scheme (TRS), where a sharp momentum cutoff is applied to both vacuum and medium contributions, with the medium separation scheme (MSS), where ultraviolet-divergent vacuum terms are isolated while finite medium-dependent phase-space contributions remain cutoff free. The two prescriptions lead to qualitatively different results. In TRS, the QSP condensate eventually collapses at high chemical potential, leading to nonmonotonic thermodynamic behavior and, for some couplings, violations of causality. In MSS, the QSP condensate instead increases monotonically with density, the equation of state remains causal, and the associated spontaneous magnetization grows toward high density. The persistence of spin polarization at high density may provide a microscopic contribution to the strong magnetic fields of compact stars, including magnetars. An exploratory hybrid-star construction further shows that the QSP phase can significantly modify the equation of state, stability trends, and mass-radius relations. These results highlight the importance of a consistent treatment of medium phase space and demonstrate the potential impact of a persistent spin-polarized quark phase on neutron-star physics.
