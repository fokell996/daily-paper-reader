## 摘要
我们提出了两种互补的神经路线，用于微观中子星状态方程推断：带混合重要性采样的截断序贯神经后验估计（TSNPE+MIS），其针对单个观测；以及带重要性采样的摊销神经后验估计（A-NET+IS），其可在不同核输入和 NICER 源之间重复使用。两者均相对于原始先验和完整似然进行校正，并以有效样本量作为诊断。我们使用核数据、NICER、GW170817 和 pQCD 检验了核子与超子相对论平均场模型。在基准分析中，两者在参数后验以及 90% 质量-半径和潮汐形变带方面均与 UltraNest 一致。TSNPE+MIS 的带边缘差异仅为 0.024–0.033 km 和 1.02–1.03%，而 A-NET+IS 的差异为 0.019–0.050 km 和 0.86–1.13%。TSNPE+MIS 将计算时间减少到原来的 1/1.6–1/2.3；训练后，A-NET+IS 对每个配置快 15–240 倍。冻结的 A-NET 还分析了训练中未出现的三个 NICER 源，包括新近报道的大质量、致密 PSR J1614-2230 后验。对于该源，推断的 R_{1.4} 与 UltraNest 在核子物质情形下相差在 0.010 km 以内，在超子物质情形下相差在 0.009 km 以内。我们还引入了格林函数证据网络（Green-function Evidence Network，EN），这是证据网络的一种基于回归的变体，据我们所知，也是首个应用于中子星状态方程推断的证据网络。在一次性开销之后，其网络查询对于新的核数据可在约 0.03 秒内返回绝对证据，对于新的 NICER 源可在 3.5 秒内返回；与 UltraNest 的全部 16 个差异均低于 1σ，且独立的重要性采样证据在所述 EN 不确定度范围内一致。在所采用的似然下，两种证据计算均以约 22:1–27:1 的贝叶斯因子支持核子物质。

## Abstract
We present two complementary neural routes for microscopic neutron-star EOS inference: truncated sequential neural posterior estimation with mixture importance sampling (TSNPE+MIS), which targets one observation, and amortized neural posterior estimation with importance sampling (A-NET+IS), which is reused across nuclear inputs and NICER sources. Both are corrected against the original prior and full likelihood, with the effective sample size as a diagnostic. We test nucleonic and hyperonic relativistic mean-field models with nuclear data, NICER, GW170817, and pQCD. For the fiducial analyses, both agree with UltraNest in the parameter posteriors and 90% mass-radius and tidal-deformability bands. TSNPE+MIS band-edge differences are only 0.024-0.033 km and 1.02-1.03%, while A-NET+IS differences are 0.019-0.050 km and 0.86-1.13%. TSNPE+MIS reduces the computing time by factors of 1.6-2.3; after training, A-NET+IS is 15-240 times faster per configuration. The frozen A-NET also analyzes three NICER sources absent from training, including the newly reported high-mass, compact PSR J1614-2230 posterior. For this source, the inferred $R_{1.4}$ agrees with UltraNest within 0.010 km for nucleonic matter and 0.009 km for hyperonic matter. We also introduce a Green-function Evidence Network (EN), a regression-based variant of Evidence Networks and, to our knowledge, the first Evidence Network applied to neutron-star EOS inference. After a one-time cost, its network queries return absolute evidences in about 0.03 s for new nuclear data and 3.5 s for a new NICER source; all 16 differences from UltraNest are below $1σ$, and independent importance-sampling evidences agree within the quoted EN uncertainties. Under the adopted likelihood, both evidence calculations favor nucleonic matter by Bayes factors of about 22:1-27:1.
