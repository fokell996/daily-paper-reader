## 摘要
热X射线脉冲轮廓通过引力光线弯曲和自转对表面辐射的影响来约束中子星的质量和半径。增强型X射线时变与偏振探测（eXTP）任务预期达到的精度，促使人们发展更精确的解析光线弯曲公式，同时保持重复模型评估所需的高效性。我们在扁椭球–史瓦西（OS）近似下推导了一个改进的解析通量表达式，它将弯曲、传播延迟和透镜近似与椭球表面结合起来。我们将它的轮廓与同一椭球表面上的数值史瓦西以及二阶Hartle–Thorne光线追踪结果进行比较。比较采用300 Hz下选定的双斑配置、各向同性黑体辐射，以及三种表面偏心率选择：自由指定值、均匀旋转牛顿流体的Maclaurin关系，以及对旋转中子星表面的拟合。在这些情形中，改进的OS轮廓相对于Hartle–Thorne参考的通量均方根差异为1.76–1.98%，而球对称史瓦西加多普勒（S+D）模型为6.50–21.39%。所有差异均以相位平均参考通量归一化。史瓦西与Hartle–Thorne光线追踪之间的相应差异保持在1.1%以下。这些比较将解析光子传输中的误差与表面形变及旋转外部时空的效应分离开来。对于所考察的构型，考虑扁率显著改善与光线追踪的一致性。该模型保留了解析计算的速度，同时明确揭示了轮廓变化的物理来源。

## 速览
**TLDR**：热X射线脉冲轮廓可通过引力弯折与自转效应约束中子星质量半径，eXTP的高精度需求推动高效且准确的解析弯折公式。本文在oblate-Schwarzschild近似下，将弯折、传播延迟、透镜效应与椭球表面结合，给出精修解析通量表达式。对300 Hz双斑黑体辐射，其轮廓与Hartle-Thorne数值光线追踪的RMS通量差仅1.76–1.98%，而球对称Schwarzschild+Doppler模型为6.50–21.39%。该模型兼顾解析速度，并明确区分表面扁率与转动时空贡献。 \
**Motivation**：eXTP等高精度X射线定时任务需要反复评估模型，因此既准确又高效的解析光弯折公式对中子星质量半径推断至关重要。 \
**Method**：在oblate-Schwarzschild近似下，结合光弯折、传播延迟、透镜效应与椭球表面推导解析通量，并与Schwarzschild及二阶Hartle-Thorne光线追踪对比。 \
**Result**：300 Hz双斑黑体下，精修OS相对Hartle-Thorne的RMS通量差1.76–1.98%，远优于球对称S+D模型的6.50–21.39%。 \
**Conclusion**：考虑扁率显著改善与光线追踪的一致性；模型保留解析计算速度，并明确轮廓变化的物理来源，适用于高效中子星参数推断。

## Abstract
Thermal X-ray pulse profiles constrain neutron-star masses and radii through the effects of gravitational light bending and rotation on surface emission. The precision anticipated from the enhanced X-ray Timing and Polarimetry (eXTP) mission motivates more accurate analytic light-bending formulae that retain the efficiency needed for repeated model evaluations. We derive a refined analytic flux expression within the oblate--Schwarzschild (OS) approximation that combines bending, propagation-delay, and lensing approximations with a spheroidal surface. We compare its profiles with numerical Schwarzschild and second-order Hartle--Thorne ray tracing on the same spheroidal surfaces. The comparisons use a selected two-spot configuration at 300 Hz with isotropic blackbody emission and three choices for surface eccentricity: a freely specified value, the Maclaurin relation for a uniformly rotating Newtonian fluid, and a fit to rotating-neutron-star surfaces. Across these cases, the refined OS profiles have RMS flux differences of $1.76-1.98\%$ relative to the Hartle--Thorne reference, compared with $6.50-21.39\%$ for the spherical Schwarzschild-plus-Doppler (S+D) model. All differences are normalized to the phase-averaged reference flux. The corresponding differences between Schwarzschild and Hartle--Thorne ray tracing remain below $1.1\%$. These comparisons separate errors in analytic photon transfer from the effects of surface deformation and the rotating exterior spacetime. For the configurations examined, accounting for oblateness substantially improves agreement with ray tracing. The model retains the speed of an analytic calculation while making the physical origin of profile changes explicit.
