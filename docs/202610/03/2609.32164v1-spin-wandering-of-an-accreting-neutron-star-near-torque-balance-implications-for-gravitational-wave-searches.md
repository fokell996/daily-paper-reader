## 摘要
引力辐射反作用与磁离心吸积之间的力矩平衡，是吸积中子星自转慢于离心破裂的原因之一。吸积盘中的随机过程，例如瑞利-泰勒不稳定性和闪烁噪声，驱使自旋频率 $f_\ast$ 以及正比于 $f_\ast$ 的引力波频率 $f_{\rm gw}$ 围绕其力矩平衡值随机游走。本文从理论上，借助理想化的 Ornstein-Uhlenbeck 模型，表明 $f_{\rm gw}$ 的标准差由 $σ_{f_{\rm gw}}(h_0) \propto f_{\rm gw} h_0 D βT^{1/2}$ 给出，或等价地由 $σ_{f_{\rm gw}}(F_{\rm X}) \propto f_{\rm gw}^{1/2} F_{\rm X}^{1/2} D βT^{1/2}$ 给出，其中 $h_0$ 是特征波应变，$F_{\rm X}$ 是 X 射线流量，$D$ 是源距离，$β$ 和 $T$ 分别是随机力矩的分数幅度和自相关时间尺度。人们可以利用 $σ_{f_{\rm gw}}(h_0)$ 和 $σ_{f_{\rm gw}}(F_{\rm X})$，通过将其与观测到的 $σ_{f_{\rm gw}}$ 进行比较，来检验引力波搜索中候选探测信号的物理自洽性。自旋游走会影响搜索设计，因为它设定了最大时间间隔 $\max(T_{\rm coh})$，在该时间间隔内信号可被视为相干的。Ornstein-Uhlenbeck 计算预测，对于瑞利-泰勒不稳定性和闪烁噪声，$10^{-1} \lesssim \max(T_{\rm coh}) / (1\, {\rm day}) \lesssim 10^3$，且 $3\times 10^{-2} \lesssim βT^{1/2} / (1 \, {\rm s^{1/2}} ) \lesssim 4$。预测的 $\max(T_{\rm coh})$ 通常短于当今运行的音频段干涉仪的为期一年的观测运行时间，这凸显了半相干搜索算法的实用性，该算法将数据划分为多个持续时间为 $\max(T_{\rm coh})$ 的相干片段。

## Abstract
Torque balance between gravitational radiation reaction and magnetocentrifugal accretion is one reason why accreting neutron stars rotate slower than centrifugal break-up. Random processes in the accretion disk, such as Rayleigh-Taylor instabilities and flicker noise, drive the spin frequency $f_\ast$ and gravitational wave frequency $f_{\rm gw}\propto f_\ast$ to wander stochastically around their torque balance values. Here it is shown theoretically, in terms of an idealized Ornstein-Uhlenbeck model, that the standard deviation of $f_{\rm gw}$ is given by $σ_{f_{\rm gw}}(h_0) \propto f_{\rm gw} h_0 D βT^{1/2}$, or equivalently $σ_{f_{\rm gw}}(F_{\rm X}) \propto f_{\rm gw}^{1/2} F_{\rm X}^{1/2} D βT^{1/2}$, where $h_0$ is the characteristic wave strain, $F_{\rm X}$ is the X-ray flux, $D$ is the source distance, and $β$ and $T$ are the fractional amplitude and autocorrelation time-scale of the stochastic torque. One can use $σ_{f_{\rm gw}}(h_0)$ and $σ_{f_{\rm gw}}(F_{\rm X})$ to check the physical consistency of a detection candidate in a gravitational wave search by comparing with $σ_{f_{\rm gw}}$ observed. Spin wandering impacts search design, because it sets the maximum time interval $\max(T_{\rm coh})$, during which the signal may be treated as coherent. The Ornstein-Uhlenbeck calculation predicts $10^{-1} \lesssim \max(T_{\rm coh}) / (1\, {\rm day}) \lesssim 10^3$, with $3\times 10^{-2} \lesssim βT^{1/2} / (1 \, {\rm s^{1/2}} ) \lesssim 4$, for Rayleigh-Taylor instabilities and flicker noise. The predicted $\max(T_{\rm coh})$ is shorter typically than the year-long observation runs of audio-band interferometers operating today, emphasizing the utility of semi-coherent search algorithms, which divide the data into multiple coherent segments of duration $\max(T_{\rm coh})$.
