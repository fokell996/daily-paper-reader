## 摘要
我们报道通过多波段跟踪观测未关联的费米大面积望远镜伽马射线源，发现了三个候选蜘蛛毫秒脉冲星。所有目标均具有来自SOAR 4.1米望远镜的光学光谱和来自Swift的X射线数据。两个光学/X射线源是极强的红背候选体，位于伽马射线源4FGL J1020.4-5314和4FGL J1408.7-3747定位椭圆的中心附近。我们表明，这两个光学源均为分光双星，其轨道周期（0.15–0.3天）和质量函数（0.6–0.9 Msun）是红背的典型值。4FGL J1020.4-5314在两年内获得的所有光谱中都显示出明亮且非常宽（半高全宽2100 km/s）的H-alpha发射。值得注意的是，H-alpha径向速度在相位上相对于伴星的吸收线速度存在偏移，并与主星附近发射源所预期的信号相符。这首次对任何蜘蛛系统证明，H-alpha发射可与包裹脉冲星的双星内激波相关联，从而为在一些红背中利用光学发射线作为双星内激波的运动学示踪体开辟了可能性。第三个候选蜘蛛与其伽马射线源4FGL J1429.8-0739的关联不太确定，但具有不寻常的性质，包括30.5小时的轨道周期、可能的亚巨星伴星和高X射线光度，值得进一步研究。

## Abstract
We report the discovery of three candidate spider millisecond pulsars via multi-wavelength follow-up of unassociated Fermi Large Area Telescope gamma-ray sources. All have optical spectroscopy from the SOAR 4.1-m telescope and X-ray data from Swift. Two optical/X-ray sources are exceptionally strong redback candidates, sitting close to the centres of the localization ellipses of the gamma-ray sources 4FGL J1020.4-5314 and 4FGL J1408.7-3747. We show that both optical sources are spectroscopic binaries with orbital periods (0.15-0.3 d) and mass functions (0.6-0.9 Msun) typical for redbacks. 4FGL J1020.4-5314 shows bright, very broad (full width at half maximum 2100 km/s) H-alpha emission in all spectra obtained over two years. Remarkably, the H-alpha radial velocities are offset in phase from the absorption-line velocities of the secondary, and match the signal expected for an emission source near the primary. This demonstrates, for the first time for any spider, that the H-alpha emission can be associated with an intrabinary shock that wraps around the pulsar, opening up the possibility of using optical emission lines as kinematic tracers of the intrabinary shock in some redbacks. A third candidate spider is less secure in its association with the gamma-ray source 4FGL J1429.8-0739, but has unusual properties, including a 30.5-hour orbital period, a potential subgiant secondary, and a high X-ray luminosity, making it worthy of further investigation.
