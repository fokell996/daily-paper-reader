## 摘要
在中子星和稠密量子色动力学（QCD）物理中，理解重子物质如何在高密度下演化为夸克物质十分重要。连续的强子—夸克渡越是极具吸引力的候选方案之一，因为它能够调和核密度附近相对软的状态方程与支撑大质量中子星所需的快速变硬。近年来的中子星观测促使人们考虑在中等密度区域快速变硬的状态方程，而夸克子物质描述在该区域则具有壳层状的重子动量分布。在本文中，我们通过与超冷费米气体和双色 QCD 中的玻色—爱因斯坦凝聚（BEC）到 Bardeen--Cooper--Schrieffer（BCS）渡越进行类比，综述我们近期对这些现象的微观描述与解释工作。由于配对涨落在 BEC—BCS 渡越中起关键作用，我们讨论其三体对应物，即与强子—夸克渡越中重子形成相关的三聚涨落的作用。借助三聚涨落的相移表示，三体束缚态极点与散射连续谱之间的相互影响抑制了低动量重子占据，产生重子动量壳层，并降低密度感受性，从而在声速中产生一个峰值。我们综述了该机制在一维三组分费米气体中的演示、其相对论推广，以及它与现象学夸克子状态方程的联系。

## Abstract
In neutron-star and dense quantum chromodynamics (QCD) physics, it is important to understand how baryonic matter evolves into quark matter at high density. A continuous hadron--quark crossover is one of the most attractive candidates because it can reconcile the comparatively soft equation of state around nuclear density with the rapid stiffening required to support massive neutron stars. Recent neutron-star observations motivate equations of state that stiffen rapidly across the intermediate-density regime, while quarkyonic-matter descriptions feature a shell-like baryon momentum distribution there. In this paper, we review our recent work on the microscopic description and interpretation of these phenomena by drawing an analogy with the Bose--Einstein condensate (BEC) to Bardeen--Cooper--Schrieffer (BCS) crossover in ultracold Fermi gases and two-color QCD. As pairing fluctuations play a crucial role in the BEC--BCS crossover, we discuss the role of its three-body counterpart, that is, tripling fluctuations associated with baryon formation in the hadron--quark crossover. In terms of a phase-shift representation of tripling fluctuations, the interplay between a three-body bound-state pole and the scattering continuum suppresses low-momentum baryonic occupation, generates a baryonic momentum shell, and reduces the density susceptibility, thereby producing a peak in the speed of sound. We review the demonstration of this mechanism in a one-dimensional three-component Fermi gas, its relativistic extension, and its connection to phenomenological quarkyonic equations of state.
