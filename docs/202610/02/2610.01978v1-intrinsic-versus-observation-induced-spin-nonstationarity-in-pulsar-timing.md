## 摘要
在双组分模型中，中子星自旋噪声是否平稳，取决于恒星角动量是在内部守恒，还是通过作用于壳层与超流体的独立随机力矩与外部交换。这个问题是以两个组分的角速度提出的，但射电脉冲星计时只能通过拟合确定性计时模型后的自转相位观测到壳层。我们发展了一种高斯过程方法，可直接利用这类相位数据检验自旋噪声平稳性。从奇异、非平稳双组分模型与最小、平稳双组分模型的精确解析相位均值和协方差出发，我们表明壳层相位协方差是半可分的，并构造了一个似然，其随观测数线性标度，且计时模型已被解析边缘化。我们使用全状态观测和仅壳层观测，并在微分为局部自旋频率之后，分析了来自这两个模型的模拟相位数据。积分、去除计时模型和微分既不会产生也不会破坏平稳与非平稳自旋噪声之间的区别，但决定了在有限、含噪数据集中有多少区别得以保留。当两个组分都被观测时，可以决定性地区分这些模型，并恢复生成参数。仅观测壳层时，两个模型在很大参数空间范围内对观测相位的描述同样好，贝叶斯因子为量级 1 且受先验限制。然而，最小模型仍通过由超流体惯性分数设定的单一因子，将隐藏的超流相位与观测到的壳层相位联系起来，因此对这一分数的独立估计（例如来自 glitch）会将其转化为一个可证伪的预言，可由对内部的连续引力波观测来检验。

## Abstract
Whether spin noise of a neutron star is stationary is, in two-component models, a question of whether stellar angular momentum is conserved internally or exchanged externally through independent stochastic torques on the crust and superfluid. The question is posed in the two components' angular velocities, but radio pulsar timing observes only the crust, through its rotational phase after a deterministic timing model is fitted. We develop a Gaussian process method for testing spin noise stationarity directly with such phase data. Starting from the exact analytical phase means and covariances of a singular, nonstationary and a minimal, stationary two-component model, we show that the crust phase covariances are semiseparable and construct a likelihood that scales linearly with the number of observations, with the timing model marginalized analytically. We analyze simulated phase data from both models using full-state and crust-only observations, and after differentiation into local spin frequencies. Integration, timing model removal and differentiation neither create nor destroy the distinction between stationary and nonstationary spin noise, but determine how much survives in a finite, noisy data set. With both components observed, the models are distinguished decisively and the generating parameters are recovered. With the crust alone, both models describe the observed phase equally well over much of parameter space, and the Bayes factors are of order unity and prior-limited. The minimal model nonetheless ties the hidden superfluid phase to the observed crust phase through a single factor set by the superfluid inertia fraction, so an independent estimate of this fraction, as from glitches, turns it into a falsifiable prediction that a continuous gravitational wave observation of the interior could test.
