## 摘要
相对论进动模型将孪生千赫兹准周期振荡解释为测试粒子在X射线双星（包含中子星或黑洞）时空中绕行的测地线频率。在若干中子星X射线双星中，准周期振荡可由几乎简并于史瓦西-德西特时空的有效几何很好地再现，这阻碍了对质量、角动量及其他可观测量进行独立测定。我们提出通过引入轨道物质自旋效应来解决这一物理局限性，最终提出了宏观进动模型。我们将盘不均匀性视为自旋测试体，由马西森-帕帕佩特罗-迪克森方程描述，并得到方位角频率和径向本征频率的非最小自旋曲率修正。我们采用基于梅特罗波利斯算法的马尔可夫链蒙特卡洛拟合，对八个中子星X射线双星源进行建模。统计分析表明，数据选择了吸积物质的内部结构，无需通过引入任何修正的德西特相位来对克尔和史瓦西时空进行修正。从物理上讲，宏观进动模型范式解释了为何在不考虑自旋时，通过非最小自旋曲率耦合，一个有效的史瓦西-德西特类结构可能在统计学上更受青睐，而测试粒子假设则保持不变。

## Abstract
The relativistic precession model (RPM) interprets the twin kilohertz quasi-periodic oscillations (QPOs) as geodesic frequencies of test particles orbiting in the spacetime of X-ray binaries hosting either a neutron star (NS) or a black hole. In several NS X-ray binaries, QPOs are well reproduced by effective geometries nearly degenerate with a Schwarzschild-de Sitter (SdS) spacetime, hindering independent determinations of the mass, the angular momentum and other observables. We propose how to solve this physical limitation by incorporating the effects of orbiting matter spin, culminating in introducing the macroscopic precession model (MPM). We treat the disk inhomogeneities as spinning test bodies governed by the Mathisson-Papapetrou-Dixon (MPD) equations and obtain non-minimal spin curvature corrections to the azimuthal and the radial epicyclic frequencies. We perform Monte Carlo Markov chain (MCMC) fits, based on the Metropolis algorithm, and model eight NS X-ray binary sources. Our statistical analyzes show that the data select the internal structure of the accreting matter, without requiring corrections to Kerr and Schwarzschild spacetimes through the introduction of any correcting de Sitter phase. Physically, the MPM paradigm explains why an effective SdS-like structure could be statistically favored if spin is not employed, through non-minimal spin-curvature coupling, leaving unaltered the test particle hypothesis.
