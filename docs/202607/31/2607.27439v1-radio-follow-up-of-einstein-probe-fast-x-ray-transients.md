## 摘要
快速X射线暂现源（FXTs）是短暂的、明亮的软X射线发射爆发，其物理起源仍不确定。爱因斯坦探针（EP）任务近来实现对这些事件的快速发现，为快速多波段后续观测提供了机会。我们介绍一项针对EP于2024年探测到的20个FXTs的协调射电观测活动。核心包括使用艾伦望远镜阵列（ATA）进行的59个历元观测，采样爆发后约1至约65天的时间尺度，并在1--8 GHz范围内达到典型的3σ灵敏度0.6--1.5 mJy。两个FXTs——EP240315a和EP241021a——具有射电对应体。对于EP241021a，AMI-LA在15.5 GHz的监测揭示了光变曲线在约30天达到峰值，F_ν≈1.0 mJy；能量均分分析表明牛顿均分能量约为3×10^49 erg，以及一个温和相对论性轴向解，Γ_on≈1.3。对EP240315a的多频建模，结合新的VLA测量与已发表的MeerKAT、ATCA和eMERLIN数据，表明高度相对论性的早期发射（Γ≳3），随时间减速，与喷流外流一致。探测结果和ATA未探测到的源的亮度范围与最亮的伽马射线暴（GRB）和相对论性潮汐瓦解事件（TDE）余辉重叠，表明FXTs包含异质群体，包括相对论性和非相对论性爆炸。在类似GRB的红移先验下，我们的3σ ATA上限对应观测者坐标系中的比光度阈值约为10^32--10^33 erg s^-1 Hz^-1；对于邻近事件（z≲0.2），L_ν≲10^30--31 erg s^-1 Hz^-1。计划中的ATA升级和下一代阵列（SKA、DSA、ngVLA）将实现对FXT射电天空的灵敏的、群体级射电研究。

## Abstract
Fast X-ray transients (FXTs) are brief, luminous bursts of soft X-ray emission whose physical origins remain uncertain. The Einstein Probe (EP) mission has recently enabled prompt discovery of these events, providing opportunities for rapid multi-wavelength follow-up. We present a coordinated radio observing campaign targeting 20 FXTs detected by the EP in 2024. The core consists of 59 epochs with the Allen Telescope Array (ATA), sampling post-burst timescales from $\sim$1 to $\sim$65 days and reaching typical $3σ$ sensitivities of 0.6--1.5\,mJy across 1--8\,GHz. Two FXTs---EP240315a and EP241021a---have radio counterparts. For EP241021a, AMI-LA monitoring at 15.5\,GHz reveals a light curve peaking at $\sim$30 days with $F_ν\approx1.0$\,mJy; equipartition analysis implies a Newtonian equipartition energy of $\sim3\times10^{49}$\,erg and a mildly relativistic on-axis solution with $Γ_{\rm on}\approx1.3$. Multi-frequency modelling of EP240315a, combining new VLA measurements with published MeerKAT, ATCA, and eMERLIN data, indicates highly relativistic early emission ($Γ\gtrsim3$) that decelerates with time, consistent with jetted outflow. The detections and ATA non-detections span luminosities overlapping the brightest GRB and relativistic tidal disruption event (TDE) afterglows, suggesting FXTs comprise a heterogeneous population including both relativistic and non-relativistic explosions. Under a GRB-like redshift prior, our $3σ$ ATA limits correspond to observer-frame specific-luminosity thresholds of $\sim10^{32}$--$10^{33}\,\mathrm{erg\ s^{-1}\ Hz^{-1}}$; for nearby events ($z\lesssim0.2$), $L_ν\lesssim10^{30\text{--}31}\,\mathrm{erg\ s^{-1}\ Hz^{-1}}$. Planned ATA upgrades and next-generation arrays (SKA, DSA, ngVLA) will enable sensitive, population-level radio studies of the FXT radio sky.
