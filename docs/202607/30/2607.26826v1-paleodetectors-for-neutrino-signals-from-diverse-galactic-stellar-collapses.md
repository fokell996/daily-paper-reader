## 摘要
研究了利用古矿物中核反冲径迹（即所谓的古探测器）来探测过去银河系核心坍缩超新星（SNe）中微子的可行性。为了考虑核心坍缩结果的多样性，考虑了中子星（NS）质量的变化以及导致黑洞（BH）形成的失败SNe。同时考虑了核状态方程（EOS）的作用，因为它决定了NS的半径和最大质量。对于中微子发射更大的模型，定量了灵敏度的提升。中微子发射反映了核心坍缩期间释放的引力能量，在形成NS的情况下，对于更致密的残骸，以及在形成BH的情况下，对于具有更大最大质量的EOS，中微子发射更大。此外，受附近SN活动可能促成了雪球地球事件的假说启发，还研究了以爆发形式发生的SNe的灵敏度。结果表明，包含数十次事件、距离10 pc的爆发式SN活动将在古探测器的探测范围内。

## Abstract
The detectability of neutrinos from past Galactic core collapse supernovae (SNe) is investigated in terms of nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals, known as paleodetectors. To account for the diversity of core-collapse outcomes, variations in neutron star (NS) masses as well as failed SNe leading to black hole (BH) formation are taken into account. The role of the nuclear equation of state (EOS) is also considered, as it determines NS radii and maximum masses. The enhancement of sensitivity is quantified for models with larger neutrino emission. This emission reflects the gravitational energy released during core collapse and is larger for more compact remnants in the NS-forming case and for EOS with larger maximum masses in the BH-forming case. Furthermore, motivated by the hypothesis that nearby SN activity may have contributed to the snowball Earth events, the sensitivity to SNe occuring in a burst-like manner is also investigated. The results indicate that burst-like SN activity involving several tens of events at a distance of 10 pc would be within the reach of paleodetectors.
