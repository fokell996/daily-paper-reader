## 摘要
核心坍缩超新星（CCSNe）多维建模的最新进展使得对高频引力波（GW）信号的详细预测成为可能，为极端物质和引力提供了新的探针。原中子星（PNS）通过激发其特征振荡模式发射准连续引力波。最新的CCSN模拟显示，这些振荡（尤其是g模和f模）主导了引力波谱，其频率随着PNS在反弹后阶段致密度的增加而从几百赫兹上升到千赫兹（kHz）范围。因此，这些引力波频率的时间演化（如果被探测到）将为PNS内部结构和周围爆炸动力学提供直接且定量的示踪。除了这种标准的引力波发射机制外，完全广义相对论（GR）模拟还揭示了与更奇异物理过程相关的额外引力波源。在高质量前身星中，持续的质量吸积导致PNS快速收缩和早期黑洞（BH）形成，产生强烈的千赫兹引力波发射，并在PNS核心被黑洞视界吞噬时突然停止。类似地，强一阶量子色动力学（QCD）相变可以诱发新生夸克核心的二次坍缩和反弹，产生持续时间毫秒、频率超过~2千赫的强大引力波暴。替代引力理论（如标量-张量框架）预测的自发标量化可能触发PNS的多次坍缩，产生类似的高频和宽带引力波信号。对这些引力波信号的综合分析，以及它们被下一代引力波探测器探测到，为识别超越CCSN引力波机制和广义相对论标准模型的新物理的确凿特征提供了一条有前景的多信道路径。

## Abstract
Recent advances in multidimensional modeling of core-collapse supernovae (CCSNe) have enabled detailed predictions of high-frequency gravitational-wave (GW) signals, offering a new probe of extreme matter and gravity. A proto-neutron star (PNS) emits quasi-continuous GWs through the excitation of its characteristic oscillation modes. State-of-the-art CCSN simulations show that these oscillations, in particular $g$- and $f$-modes, dominate the GW spectrum, with frequencies rising from a few hundred hertz to the kilohertz (kHz) range as the PNS compactness increases in the post-bounce phase. Therefore, the temporal evolution of these GW frequencies, if detected, would provide a direct and quantitative tracer of the PNS internal structure and the surrounding explosive dynamics. In addition to such standard GW emission mechanism, fully general relativistic (GR) simulations have revealed additional GW sources linked to more exotic physical processes. In highly massive progenitors, continuous mass accretion drives rapid PNS contraction and early black-hole (BH) formation, producing strong kHz GW emission that abruptly ceases when the PNS core is swallowed by the BH horizon. Similarly, a strong first-order quantum chromodynamics (QCD) phase transition can induce a secondary collapse and rebound of the nascent quark core, generating powerful, millisecond-duration GW bursts with frequencies exceeding $\sim$2 kHz. Alternative theories of gravity, such as scalar-tensor frameworks, predict spontaneous scalarization that can trigger multiple collapses of the PNS, yielding analogous high-frequency and broadband GW signals. The combined analysis of these GW signals, together with their detection by next-generation GW detectors, offers a promising multi-messenger pathway to identify smoking-gun signatures of new physics beyond the standard model of the CCSN GW mechanism and general relativity.
