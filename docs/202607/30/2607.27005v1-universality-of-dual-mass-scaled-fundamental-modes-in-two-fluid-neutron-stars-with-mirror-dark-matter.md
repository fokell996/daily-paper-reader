## 摘要
普适关系提供了一种特别有用的方式，在存在各种不确定性的情况下，通过减少对不确定模型参数和微观物理输入的依赖，从中子星可观测物中提取物理信息。在本研究中，我们使用双流体描述检查了镜像暗物质混合中子星的振荡频率，其中外层和内层流体产生两种不同的基频。我们确认了之前对于自相互作用暗物质混合中子星建立的、由外层流体主导的模的质量标度基频与恒星致密度之间的普适关系，在镜像暗物质场景中也成立。然而，与Cowling近似下的相应结果相比，当包含度规扰动时，这种普适关系变得不那么稳健。我们进一步发现，内层流体主导的基频也可以表示为与致密度相关的关系，该关系在很大程度上与正常物质状态方程无关，前提是暗物质质量分数固定。这些结果表明，即使正常物质的状态方程仍不确定，同时探测两个基频可以提供一种约束暗物质质量分数的方法。最后，我们发现Cowling近似估计与外层流体相关的基频的精度与没有暗物质的标准中子星相当，而对于内层流体相关的频率，其表现甚至更好。

## Abstract
Universal relations provide a particularly useful way to extract physical information from neutron star observables in the presence of various uncertainties by reducing the dependence on uncertain model parameters and microphysical inputs. In this study, we examine the oscillation frequencies of mirror dark matter admixed neutron stars using a two-fluid description, where the outer and inner fluids give rise to two distinct fundamental frequencies. We confirm that the universal relation between the mass-scaled fundamental frequency of the outer-fluid-led mode and the stellar compactness, established previously for self-interacting dark matter admixed neutron stars, also holds in the mirror dark matter scenario. However, this universal relation becomes less robust when metric perturbations are included, compared with the corresponding results in the Cowling approximation. We further find that the inner-fluid-led fundamental frequency can also be expressed as a compactness-dependent relation that is largely independent of the normal matter equation of state, provided that the dark matter mass fraction is fixed. These results suggest that the simultaneous detection of the two fundamental frequencies could provide a way to constrain the dark matter mass fraction, even when the equation of state of normal matter remains uncertain. Finally, we find that the Cowling approximation estimates the fundamental frequency associated with the outer fluid with an accuracy comparable to that found for standard neutron stars without dark matter, while it performs even better for the frequency associated with the inner fluid.
