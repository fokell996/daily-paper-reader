## 摘要
过渡毫秒脉冲星（tMSPs）是中子星双星系统，它们在旋转供能和吸积供能状态之间切换，为低质量X射线双星和毫秒射电脉冲星之间提供了关键联系。在其亚发光盘态下，这些系统表现出复杂的变异性，其起源仍有争议。我们展示了在亚发光盘态下获得的tMSP PSR J1023+0038的高时间分辨率光学光谱观测数据。我们的数据集首次以分钟量级的时间分辨率覆盖了完整的轨道周期。我们检测到光学发射线主要性质（包括等效宽度（EW）和半高全宽（FWHM））在分钟尺度上的显著变化。对这些量随时间演化的比较揭示了相关行为的迹象，一些FWHM最小值与EW的下降同时发生。这可能指向吸积盘内部区域物质抛射的事件，可能与切换至低模式有关。Hα和Hβ发射线的多普勒断层扫描表明存在不对称发射结构，这与部分吸积物质从系统中被抛出的情景一致。此外，光学连续谱显示出与经照射的伴星可能的轨道调制一致的变异性，尽管其表征受到观测条件的限制。我们的结果为亚发光盘态下tMSPs的短时标行为提供了新的约束，并支持吸积与外流过程共存的情景。进一步的多波段观测，特别是同时包含X射线覆盖，对于建立观测到的光学变异性与高/低模式切换之间的直接联系至关重要。

## Abstract
Transitional millisecond pulsars (tMSPs) are neutron-star binaries that switch between rotation-powered and accretion-powered states, providing a key link between low-mass X-ray binaries and millisecond radio pulsars. In their sub-luminous disc state, these systems exhibit complex variability whose origin is still debated. We present high-time-resolution optical spectroscopic observations of the tMSP PSR J1023+0038 obtained during its sub-luminous disc state. Our dataset covers for the first time a full orbital cycle at minute-timescale cadence. We detect significant variability in the main properties of the optical emission lines, including the equivalent width (EW) and full width at half maximum (FWHM), on timescales of minutes. A comparison between the temporal evolution of these quantities reveals indications of correlated behaviour, with some FWHM minima coinciding with decreases in the EW. This may point to episodes of matter ejection from the inner regions of the accretion disc, possibly associated with switches to low modes. The Doppler tomography of the H$α$ and H$β$ emission lines suggests the presence of asymmetric emission structures, consistent with a scenario in which part of the accreting material is expelled from the system. In addition, the optical continuum shows variability consistent with a possible orbital modulation associated with the irradiated companion star, although its characterisation is limited by the observing conditions. Our results provide new constraints on the short-timescale behaviour of tMSPs in the sub-luminous disc state and support scenarios in which accretion and outflow processes coexist. Further multiwavelength observations, particularly including simultaneous X-ray coverage, will be crucial to establish a direct link between the observed optical variability and the high/low mode switches.

## 6. 论文的主要结论与发现

1. **发射线EW和FWHM存在分钟级相关变化**：一些FWHM最小值同时伴随EW下降（如相位~0.6和~1.1），暗示高速发射物质（内盘区域）暂时减弱或消失，可能对应X射线低模式时内盘被抛射。
2. **多普勒层析显示非对称发射结构**：Hα和Hβ的发射主要分布在负v_x和负v_y象限，类似XTE J2123–058和SW Sex型激变变星，可能源于螺旋桨机制导致的抛射物再撞击盘。

4. **色-星等图显示亮度越低颜色越红**：可能与内盘部分排空有关。
5. **未能检测到伴星发射成分**：窄线分离和Doppler图均未显示伴星发射特征，因此EW最大值（相位~1）并非源于伴星。
6. **未发现明显的P Cygni轮廓**：由于分辨率限制（6.5 Å），不能排除风的存在。

## 8. 不足与局限：包括实验覆盖、偏差风险、应用限制等

- **应用限制**：结果仅适用于PSR J1023+0038，以及其特定的亚发光盘态（低吸积率）。向其他tMSP推广需谨慎。
